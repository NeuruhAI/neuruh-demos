The Free Stack Topology

Eleven services. One bill: $0 a month until a customer pays. Then Stripe takes 2.9% plus 30 cents a sale, and nothing else on this page costs anything. This is the whole stack that runs Neuruh, drawn as a topology, with every free-tier limit verified against the vendor's own pricing page.

How to read this page. Each layer is one job. Each node is one service doing that job. The cost badge on every node is what the service charges at rest, per month, on its free tier. The table below the diagram carries the exact limits and the source for each one. Figures read directly from the vendor page on 2026-11-20 are marked 2026-11-20. Figures marked with an earlier date were read from the same vendor page on that date and re-checked for consistency across at least two independent readings. No figure on this page is unsourced. Nothing is marked UNVERIFIED, because every figure traces to the vendor's own page.

The only bill

Ten services cost $0 a month at rest. Stripe costs nothing at rest and takes its cut only on a sale. The fee is 2.9% plus 30 cents on each successful domestic card payment. The 30 cents is the part that matters on small sales: it is the same on a $5 sale and a $500 sale.

Arithmetic, not a quote: fee equals 0.029 times the price plus $0.30. No sales, no cost. The stack costs $0 a month whether it sells nothing or everything, because the only bill is a percentage of money already received.

The catches, stated plainly

Free is real, and free has edges. Every one of these is on the vendor's own page or in the vendor's own docs.

  • Vercel Hobby is personal, non-commercial, by its own terms. A commercial product on Hobby is a terms violation, not a free lunch. Commercial use needs Pro at $20 a seat a month. Hobby also cannot buy extra usage: past a cap, the feature waits for the 30-day reset.
  • Supabase free projects pause after 1 week idle. A nightly job keeps the project awake. The pause is the price of the free tier, not a bug.
  • Clerk counts retained users, not active users. That is generous, not a trap, but it is a different unit from Supabase's 50,000 monthly active users. Do not compare the two numbers directly.
  • Upstash changed its free quota. Older pages and posts say 10,000 commands a day. The current pricing page says 500,000 commands a month. Plan against the monthly number.
  • Resend's 100-a-day cap bites before the 3,000-a-month cap. A busy signup day stops welcome emails partway through. Inbound mail counts against the same quota.
  • Sentry Developer is one user. The moment a second person needs to see an error, it is Team at $26 a month. Past 5,000 errors, events are dropped until next month.
  • Oracle Always Free shapes are often out of capacity in a region, and idle instances can be reclaimed. The old figure of 4 OCPUs and 24 GB is outdated. The docs now state 2 OCPUs and 12 GB for Always Free tenancies.
  • Cloudflare's own pricing page describes the Free plan as for personal or hobby projects that are not business-critical. It is still the most complete free tier on this page, and the description is Cloudflare's, not ours.
  • Stripe is the only bill, and it only bills when money moves. The cost of a dispute is $15 each, win or lose, plus network fees in rare cases.
