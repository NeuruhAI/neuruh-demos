1 Swarm Topology: Seven Legions

Every one of the 179 sites lives in exactly one legion. Click a legion to filter the registry below to its systems. The legions are the swarm's organs; the mission loop is the blood.

2 Run a Mission: The Ten-Stage Autonomous Loop

Pick a threat domain, paste a signal, run the mission. The swarm walks it through all ten stages on its own: intake, team formation, execution, deliberation, gating, handoff, learning, proof, factory, canon. Each stage names the real systems it invokes and leaves a chained receipt. A stage can BLOCK the mission, and the block is itself receipted. The loop stops before production promotion: the action it drafts is gated output, and a human approves the send.

The mission ID is the first 12 characters of the SHA-256 of the signal. The receipt chain starts at GENESIS and every stage receipt covers the previous stage's hash.
