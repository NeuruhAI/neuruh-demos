01What the lab is

The atlas stated the six problems and named six computable first moves. The lab is where those moves get executed. Each move is a real program with a real run, a real result file, and a hash-chained receipt, so anyone can re-run it and check it. Round 1 ran the two calibration moves and named the four hunts. Round 2 ran all four hunts, plus calibration extensions: BSD to conductor 30 and Riemann to 2,000 zeros. Its headline results: Yang-Mills three-point torelon extrapolation intercept 3.473 plus or minus 1.506 with the slope error 500 times the slope, supporting nothing; Navier-Stokes Kida-Pelz at 643 grew enstrophy 18.86 and stayed smooth, growth saturating with resolution; P versus NP defeater-free fragment decided in polynomial time by a node-wise greedy search; Hodge group-level exponential sequence formalized, sheaf O_X assessed not tractable. Rounds 3 and 4 ran simultaneously, nine moves in total: four hunts in each round plus a round 4 calibration extension. Round 5 ran five moves: the fifth beta point at 2.6, lower viscosity at nu 0.00025, the wider P versus NP fragment, the sheaf long exact sequence with c1, and calibration extensions to conductor 50 and 4,000 zeros. A move counts only when its receipts exist.

The lab ledger is a separate store (data/lab.db) in the same format. The chain was verified after the store was closed and reopened, which is the restart proof. 02Riemann: first 4,000 zeros, re-verified

RUN November 21, 2026, round 5 calibration. Method: PARI/GP 2.19.1 lfunzeros locates every zero with imaginary part in [0, 4650] (C implementation, Riemann-Siegel, argument principle). Then, for each located t, an independent mpmath Newton step from 0.5 + i t using zeta and zeta prime, at 30 decimal digits. The Newton step is free to move off the line; the reported real part is where it converges. The two implementations agree: the first zero from PARI matches the mpmath bisection value to 22 digits.

Result: 4000 zeros checked, first at t = 14.1347, last at t = 4506.3115. Largest deviation of a zero real part from 1/2: 0.0 at 30-digit precision (no zero deviated: every Newton step returned Re = 1/2 exactly at this precision, so the deviation is below 1e-30). Largest |zeta(1/2 + i t)| at a located zero: 7.4547e-21. Count check against the Riemann-von Mangoldt formula at T: expected 3999.53 zeros, found 4,000, so S(T) = 0.469, inside the small range the formula allows. The first 3,000 t values match round 4 exactly. The new interval [3545, 4650] holds 984 zeros, and with the 16 leftover zeros from the round 4 upper scan that is 1,000 new zeros beyond round 4. The new scan ran twice with identical hashes; the second run took 414.0 seconds and the mpmath verification pass took 560.91 seconds. The full [0, 4650] scan overflows the default 8 MB PARI stack, so the interval was split, the same workaround recorded in round 2.

This is a re-verification, not a new record. The first 1013 zeros were already verified on the line by others. This run checks the first 4,000 again, independently, on this machine, with receipts.

06P versus NP round 3: the full fragment with defeaters is polynomial

RUN November 20, 2026. Repo NeuruhAI/lead-recipe-os, HEAD 7363dcb1, fetched read-only. Round 2 decided the defeater-free fragment in polynomial time and left the full fragment, with defeaters, as the open attack surface. Round 3 attacks it.

The defeater rule makes the decision easier, not harder. The dichotomy was verified exhaustively: 8,876 checks across 14 timelines, 317 signals, two floor settings, 0 violations. For every signal and every timeline, exactly one of leaf(s) and NOT(defeater leaf(s)) is SATISFIED. A SATISFIED node therefore always exists, and node-wise greedy search decides FULL-COMP in polynomial time: a valid witness was built on 14 of 14 timelines, candidate pool 3170 to 3867, 0.33 to 1.82 seconds per run. Brute-force agreement: 47 of 47 on the full fragment, 44 of 44 replicating the round 2 defeater-free check.

Hardness direction, fails on all three defeater encodings: a formula tree can distinguish SAT from UNSAT on a fixed assignment, but the search never has to use it, because a trivially SATISFIED node always exists elsewhere. One edge case is load-bearing: a hand-built row with signal_logic None and a required signal validates and evaluates SATISFIED on every timeline, but it is a META recipe, excluded from FULL-COMP by definition. The exclusion is what keeps the fragment honest.

Verdict: the full fragment admits an explicit polynomial-time search algorithm, validated against brute force. Nothing here separates or collapses P and NP, and none is claimed. The hardness question for this object is structurally impossible in the direction attempted.

07P versus NP round 4: the phase transition does not survive defeaters

RUN November 20, 2026. Repo NeuruhAI/lead-recipe-os, HEAD 7363dcb1, fetched read-only. Round 1 measured a sharp phase transition in the registry: match fraction tracking pr against evidence density. Round 4 measures it again in the full fragment with defeaters, 200 trials per cell.

Experiment A, 140 cells: plain-only recipes track pr; defeater-only recipes track (1-p)r, the mirror image, easiest to match at low density; a 50/50 mixed recipe tracks 0.5r at every density, so density stops mattering at all; a 25 percent defeater mix tracks (0.25 + 0.5p)r. Largest deviation from prediction across all 140 cells: 0.065. Experiment A2, evidential absence, 56 cells: NOT(plain) and the ABSENT operator track (0.5(1-p))r; NOT(defeater) tracks the full (1-p)r. Largest deviation: 0.055.

Experiment B, existence against density: no rare regime exists. A SATISFIED composed recipe exists on 1400 of 1400 trials at every density from p = 0.0 to 1.0, found in 1.0 to 2.0 node checks per trial. The witness kind flips with density: NOT(defeater) at p = 0, plain loose leaves at p = 1.0.

Greedy correctness argument: a proof sketch for DF-COMP-SEARCH is written up in data/p_vs_np_greedy_proof_r4.md. The theorem is that DF-COMP-SEARCH is decidable in polynomial time by one pass over a candidate node pool of at most 2,599 nodes, because ALL is SATISFIED exactly when every child is, each child evaluates independently, every composer node form is covered by the pool with dominating parameters, and a witness is then built with the real composer and validator. The scope is stated honestly: the lemmas prove the evaluator question exactly; the validity-aware answer is sound by construction and complete on all 9 battery timelines; it does not extend to arbitrary LogicNode trees or to the full fragment, where existence degenerates to always YES. One round 2 overclaim is corrected here: loose-floor dominance is not a theorem of best(), because the pool tests both floor settings.
