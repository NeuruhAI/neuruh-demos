Particle Playground

Browser version of the source spec: a particle system that attracts and repels around a live point, with trails, color cycling, and a golden-ratio distribution. The source built this in Colab with MediaPipe and OpenCV; this page runs the same idea natively in the browser. Move the pointer to steer the field. Click for a burst. Turn on the camera to drive the attractor with your hand position (no video leaves the device).

How it maps to the source

The source spec checks feasibility of MediaPipe hand/face landmarks in Colab, webcam frames bridged by JavaScript, a NumPy particle system that attracts or repels around detected landmarks, OpenCV rendering, and themes such as color cycling on a fist gesture, trails, and golden-ratio distribution. This page keeps the particle behavior and the themes, and replaces the Colab bridge with a canvas plus pointer input. Camera mode uses the browser camera only as a motion reference: it tracks the brightest moving region and moves the attractor there. If the camera is unavailable or denied, pointer mode keeps working.

Controls

Attract pulls particles toward the pointer; Repel pushes them away. Click or tap anywhere for a burst. Golden-ratio spiral re-seeds particles along a phyllotaxis spiral. Scatter re-seeds at random. Trails fade the previous frame instead of clearing it.
