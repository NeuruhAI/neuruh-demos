Video Script 3: The Org Chart Is the Product

Talking-head script, 60-90 seconds. One idea: nobody buys the agent team, they buy the proof. Plain spoken language.

HOOK (first 3 seconds): Nobody buys your agent team. They buy the proof it worked.

BODY:

In 2025 I sat in demo rooms and watched beautiful multi-agent demos die the same way. Gorgeous orchestration, nodding prospect, stalled deal. So I asked the stalled prospects what actually mattered.

Nobody asked to buy the agents. They asked three questions. Who decided this thing was allowed to act? What did it touch, spend, and produce? And can you prove it did what you said it did?

So here is the discipline I run now. For every job, I compile the smallest team that can do it. Fewer agents, fewer conversations between them, and a human gate before anything goes out. What executes is only what survives three checks: what I proposed, what policy permits, and what the infrastructure can actually do right now.

Then I keep the receipt. The org chart that did the work, who approved what, what got blocked and why, what it cost, and what it actually produced.

The agents are cheap. The receipts are the asset. That is Book 3 of The Neuruh Papers. Link is below.
