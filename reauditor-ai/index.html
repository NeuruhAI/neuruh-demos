ReAuditor AI

Photograph a property. Get the repairs, the costs, the after-repair value, and the strategy. This page runs the three-step flow from the July 2025 storyboard exactly as specified: upload photos, AI analysis, report.

Simulation, not a live product. ReAuditor AI is a July 2025 spec (DESIGNED, not live, not deployed, not proven). Every finding, cost, and figure on this page is a worked example drawn from the spec docs and the demo video frame (repair cost $15,000). Nothing here is a computed estimate for a real property, and no AI analysis runs in your browser. The demo shows what the spec promises, so the promise can be inspected.

How it works

The three steps, verbatim from the storyboard Doc, "Update, ReAuditor AI, Storyboard, How It Works".

STEP 1

Upload Photos

Take photos or upload existing images of your property using our mobile app or web interface.

STEP 2

AI Analysis

Our advanced AI analyzes your images to identify repairs, calculate costs, and estimate property value.

STEP 3

Get Report

Receive detailed reports with repair estimates, ARV calculations, and investment insights.
