03Auction and redemption rules for the jurisdiction

04Value, repairs, minimum bid

Value gate. Resale value is the after-repair value of the property, from comps, not from the auction listing. Repairs are a scope, not a guess. The minimum bid is the auction opening bid from the listing. Every dollar figure below is a MODELED ESTIMATE, NOT A VALUATION, NOT AN APPRAISAL.

Warning: senior liens are entered as 0 but no lien search is recorded in the notes. Confirm the lien search was actually done, or write N/A in the notes and treat the run as HOLD.

05Max bid

Deterministic math. Max bid is the highest hammer bid that still leaves the stated profit after repairs, holding, title, and senior liens. Senior liens are paid on top of the bid, so they come out of the max bid dollar for dollar. Every dollar figure below is a MODELED ESTIMATE, NOT A VALUATION, NOT AN APPRAISAL.

Max Bid = Resale Value - Repairs - Holding Total - Title Costs Total - Desired Profit - Senior Liens
Headroom = Max Bid - Minimum Bid
Desired Profit = Resale Value x Profit Percent / 100
Max bid is the hammer bid before any buyer premium. Buyer premium is shown separately in section 06.

06Redemption risk

Redemption math. If the owner redeems, the bidder gets the bid back plus interest, and keeps none of the property upside. The model is conservative: redemption payoff is bid plus interest only, and costs paid at purchase are not reimbursed. Every dollar figure below is a MODELED ESTIMATE, NOT A VALUATION, NOT AN APPRAISAL.

Interest Earned = Planned Bid x Redemption Rate / 100 x Redemption Months / 12
Redemption Payoff = Planned Bid + Interest Earned
Net if Redeemed = Interest Earned - (Monthly Holding x Redemption Months) - Costs at Purchase - Buyer Premium
Net if Kept = Resale Value - Repairs - Holding Total - Title Costs Total - Planned Bid - Buyer Premium
Expected Value = (1 - Redemption Probability) x Net if Kept + Redemption Probability x Net if Redeemed

14How it works / What this is not / Sources


Read this before relying on any number on this page.


How it works: confirm the parcel from at least 2 public sources, verify the auction and redemption rules for the county from the county or statute source, enter resale value from comps, repairs, title costs, and monthly holding costs. The tool computes holding total, title total, desired profit, max bid, headroom, redemption payoff, net if redeemed, net if kept, and expected value from the exact formulas in sections 05, 06, and 08. Gates fail closed. A kill means PASS. The receipt records what was entered, what passed, and what was N/A. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers. The working-lines block in section 05 shows the live max bid arithmetic for the current inputs.


What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not legal or tax advice, and not a title opinion. It does not check the auction listing for you, does not confirm the redemption period, does not run a title search, and does not guarantee the property is vacant, the title is insurable, or the owner will not redeem. It does not invent rules, prices, comps, or source claims. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. No bid is placed without Jeramie.

14Honest gaps and limits


What this tool does not do, stated plainly.


    

  • This engine does not pull live county data: every rule, bid, and lien must be typed in from a source you checked. It does not model bidding competition, so a max bid is a ceiling, not a prediction of the winning bid. It does not model senior lien priority beyond the dollar amount you enter; lien priority is a title question. It does not model eviction cost or timeline beyond the possession plan note. Redemption probability is your estimate, not a measured rate; expected value moves with it, so state the basis. Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Sample deals are synthetic and labeled as such; they use example county names and example source references, not real records.
    • 

  • Fixes over the 2026-11-04 page script, recorded here: (1) The auto kill checkboxes (title uninsurable, max bid below minimum) are now checked by the page when their conditions are met. In the 2026-11-04 build the labels said "(checked automatically when the condition is met)" but calc() never set the boxes, so the kill fired in the recommendation while its box stayed unchecked. The recommendation logic is unchanged; only the displayed checkbox state now matches it. (2) A dead line referencing a nonexistent element id "promptView" was removed from the 2026-11-04 script; it was a harmless no-op. The system prompt is now shown in the section 15 details block instead.
    • 

  • Quirk carried verbatim: monthly holding is always a number, even $0.00 on an empty form, because a blank monthly line counts as 0 in the 2026-11-04 formula. Holding total is still N/A until redemption months are entered, and max bid is still N/A until every one of its six inputs is entered, including blank repairs. A farm of blank lines does not produce a max bid.
    • 

  • Sample C fires three kill reasons, not two: the two contract kills (redemption period unknown, title uninsurable with no quiet-title budget) plus the operator-checked rules kill (auction rules unverified). The 2026-11-04 sample note says "both contract kills fire", which is true for the two contract kills; the third reason is the checked rules box firing as written.
    • 

  • Title total is N/A only when all four title parts are blank, and costs at purchase is N/A only when search, recording, and closing are all blank. A blank quiet-title budget counts as no budget for the title kill, so an uninsurable title with a blank quiet-title budget is a kill, not a hold.
