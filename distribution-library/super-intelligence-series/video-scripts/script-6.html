Book 6 Video Script: The Sovereign Assistant (60-90 sec)

One idea: the one app never shipped, but the parts finally exist, so assemble your own.

Nobody is shipping the AI app you're waiting for. The one that runs your whole digital life. And that is good news, once you see why.

In 2025 I designed it on paper. Two hundred sections, a full cognitive operating system. It stayed on paper because the pieces weren't standard yet.

In late 2026, they are. The connectivity layer got standardized. The orchestration layer matured. Memory fits on a laptop. Sandboxes keep the machine safe.

Here's the architecture, in plain words. Don't build one agent. Build an organization. One supervisor that plans, delegates, and recovers when things go wrong. And four specialists under it: one owns your memory, one owns your schedule and email, one runs code in a sandbox, and one clicks whatever's left behind a login screen.

And the reason this beats the app you were waiting for: the app would have needed your email, your chats, your drive, your calendar, all in one breachable place. Your version runs on your machine. You hold the data. You hold the keys.

The bus already left. But it was never a bus. It was a bus route. Build your own stops.
