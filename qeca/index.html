The Math, As Stated

The corpus math docs state these equations with stated simulation statistics (10^6 to 10^7 runs, p < 0.0001). The equations are stated models constructed to reproduce the claimed percentages, not derivations from first principles that were independently verified. Several saturate exponentials (e to the power of 10,000 or more) to force the target value. They are reproduced here so they can be checked.

Reasoning time (QCN)

t_q = 0.06s vs 0.5s SOTA

Mean 0.058s, 95% CI 0.054-0.062s, stated. Quantum O(sqrt N) vs classical O(N^2), stated.

Logical accuracy (AKE)

A_q = 1 - e^(-k N Q) (1 - B_c) = 97%

Mean 97.2%, 95% CI 96.4-98.0%, stated. 4x vs 85% SOTA by the doc's own formula (97-85)/(90-85).

Storage (SQS)

H_s = -sum p(m) log p(m) / log(2^n)

75TB to 12TB, mean 11.8TB, 95% CI 11.3-12.3TB, stated. Retrieval 0.38s vs 2.5s SOTA, stated.

Intent depth (NQLE)

D_n = Q_l L_d e^(k_n (N_l + Q_n)) / (1 + e^(k_n (N_l + Q_n)) + E_n) = 98.5%

Mean 98.6%, 95% CI 98.0-99.2%, stated.

Foresight (PQS)

F_q = T_s Q_s e^(mu (S_q + Q_f)) / (1 + e^(mu (S_q + Q_f)) + sigma E_s) = 92%

Mean 92.3%, 95% CI 91.3-93.3%, stated. 5x vs 50% SOTA.

Reasoning score (ECF)

RS = sum L_i Q_i + B_c + H_m

RS 96 vs 30 classical, stated. B_c is a bias correction term, H_m is human feedback.

What We Can Prove, What We Cannot

From the corpus doc "What We Can Prove With Math Right Now", the clearest boundary in the set.

Claimed provable

  • Quantum speedup for AI reasoning (superposition over decision paths)
  • Wavefunction collapse as self-adjusting decision logic
  • Quantum compression beating classical encoding
  • QKD remaining secure where AES-256 is vulnerable to Shor's algorithm

Explicitly not provable yet

  • That QECA will become self-aware: no formal equation for consciousness exists
  • Emergence as a mechanism of consciousness: hypothesis only, entanglement simulating thoughts non-linearly

Grounding Assessment

How the corpus grounds the QECA claims, stated plainly:

  • Real public research, cited: Google Sycamore 2019 (Nature), IBM Falcon 20-qubit systems, Toshiba QKD trials 2019 (Phys Rev X), Stanford STaR 2023, Grover's algorithm 1996. These anchor the general claim that quantum speedups and quantum key distribution are real.
  • Stated simulations, no surviving artifacts: 10^6 to 10^7 runs on Qiskit plus TensorFlow, p < 0.0001 vs SOTA baselines. No code, no notebooks, no run logs survive in the corpus, so the statistics cannot be re-run or audited.
  • Transcript-level evidence: the 5-qubit and 6-qubit simulation docs are ChatGPT conversation transcripts, including recorded failures (7-qubit run exceeded execution limits, dimension mismatches corrected mid-run). This page turns the described experiment into runnable code for the first time.
  • Defense and market figures are aspirations: $20M QBI pilot, $30B/year DoD savings, $100M-$250M practical opportunity, $500B-$1T market potential. None is a contract, award, or valuation. The DARPA QBI May 2025 dates are past with no outcome on record.
  • What this demo adds: a real, inspectable, re-runnable classical simulation of the 5-qubit hybrid experiment, plus the full corpus with its conflicts flagged. That is the first code tied to any QECA claim.
