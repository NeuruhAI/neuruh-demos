05Operating expenses

NOI, monthly = Effective gross income - Operating expenses. Debt service is not an operating expense.

06Refinance math

Refinance loan is ARV times LTV. Net refinance proceeds pay off the initial loan and refinance closing costs. Cash left in deal is total cash invested minus net refinance proceeds.

Refinance loan = ARV x LTV %
Monthly payment = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where r = annual rate / 12 and n = term in years x 12. If rate is 0, payment = P / n.
Net refinance proceeds = Refinance loan - Initial loan payoff - Refinance closing costs
Cash left in deal = Total cash invested - Net refinance proceeds

08DSCR, break-even occupancy, and returns

DSCR and break-even occupancy use base-scenario NOI and the refinance payment. If the refinance payment is N/A, DSCR and break-even occupancy are N/A.

DSCR = Annual NOI (base) / Annual debt service (refinance payment x 12)
Break-even occupancy = (Annual operating expenses + Annual debt service) / Gross scheduled annual rent
Cap rate = Annual NOI (base) / All-in cost
Cash-on-cash return = Annual cash flow (base) / Cash left in deal, only when cash left in deal is greater than 0. If cash left is 0 or negative, cash-on-cash is N/A because the capital is already out.

13How it works / What this is not / Sources

Read this before relying on any number on this page.

How it works: confirm the address and parcel from at least 2 public sources, grade the market rent with rent comps and a named source, enter purchase price, ARV, and a repair scope, enter operating expenses, then enter refinance LTV, rate, and term. The tool computes effective gross income, NOI, cash flow in three scenarios, refinance loan and payment, cash left in deal, DSCR, break-even occupancy, cap rate, and cash-on-cash return from the exact formulas shown. Gates fail closed. A kill means PASS. The receipt records what was entered, what passed, and what was N/A. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL hash so someone else can open the same numbers in their own browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not legal, tax, or lending advice, and not a guarantee of rent, ARV, repairs, refinance terms, or a sale. It does not invent rents, comps, ARV, or loan terms. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. A lender will underwrite DSCR, LTV, and reserves on its own terms, and those terms can differ from the ones entered here. No offer is quoted without Jeramie.

14Honest gaps and limits

What this tool does not do, stated plainly.

  • It does not verify ARV. ARV entered here is a claim until supported by sold comps in the Residential engine or an appraisal.
  • It does not verify rent. A C-grade market estimate and a D-grade seller claim are not proven income. Only a signed lease is proven rent.
  • It does not price title, insurance, tax, or legal risk. A title defect, a tax lien, or a floodplain flag needs the E8 Distressed Title engine and a licensed professional.
  • It does not model taxes, depreciation, or after-tax return. Cash flow here is pre-tax.
  • It does not model rent growth, expense inflation, interest rate changes after refinance, or vacancy beyond the three fixed scenarios.
  • It assumes the refinance loan is a fully amortizing fixed-rate loan with the entered rate and term. It does not model adjustable rates, interest-only periods, balloon payments, points, or lender reserves.
  • It assumes the initial loan payoff equals the entered initial loan balance. If the payoff includes prepayment penalties, accrued interest, or a second lien, enter the full payoff in that field or the cash left number will be wrong.
  • It does not check local short-term rental rules, HOA rental caps, rent control, or eviction law. A property that cannot legally be rented at the stated rent is HOLD, not a low price.
  • No E3 specification document was found in the vault. Greps for E3, Rental, and BRRRR across the vault returned only the UnderwriteX.md line cited in section 13, the 2026-11-04 E3 build itself, and unrelated hits (flowchart node IDs, T4 E3 reports). The Codex V2 Appendix B cited as lineage in the 2026-11-04 build was not located in the vault. What would ground E3 further: the Codex V2 Appendix B document, or a written E3 spec from Jeramie stating the subject and mechanics.
  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Math was tested in Node with hand-computed values, plus a jsdom DOM smoke test. See the build report.
