Yes. I went through the package structure and enough of the actual tokens/prototypes to establish the hierarchy. These should now function as design custody, not just inspiration.

What I'm treating as effectively locked

  1. Master authority: Neuruh Master Design System.zip is the strongest source. It literally identifies itself as the source of truth for the flagship work, products, evidence/trust, and Blackbox diagnostics.

  2. Neuruh.com flagship direction: Neuruh.com : Direction A Prototype.zip is materially more important than the three-direction exploration because Direction A was carried forward into a dedicated prototype. The thesis is Biological-Architectural: engineered tissue, membranes, evidence channels, a hard crystalline governance core, and receipts returning through the system. That gives us a real spatial metaphor instead of generic "AI graphics."

  3. Brand architecture: "Share the foundation. Never share the face." Products can share infrastructure, typography roles, evidence semantics, governance semantics, accessibility, components, etc. They should not all look like reskinned Neuruh.com.

  4. Core visual grammar: - Obsidian: #0B0D12 canonical dark - Bone: #FAF9F6 principal warm light - Brass: approximately #DDBA6A / #B08535 for earned illumination/authority - Verified = spectral green - Review/uncertainty = amber - Contradiction/failure = crimson - Product verticals can introduce controlled mineral cyan / indigo / violet / clay accents. The critical rule is that brass means something. It is not decorative gold.

  5. Two-spine strategy: dark is not "the Neuruh look." - Obsidian = depth, authority, cinematic environments, diagnostics - Bone = evidence, explanation, editorial surfaces, reports, client-facing systems That prevents everything from becoming another black hacker dashboard.

  6. Typography: - Archivo = display / major operational headings - Inter = primary UI/body - JetBrains Mono = machine evidence only: IDs, hashes, receipts, coordinates, telemetry, timestamps, system labels Mono prose is specifically forbidden.

  7. Material strategy: physical, architectural, mineral, engineered. Not glossy generic SaaS. Surfaces should feel like: - machined plates - mineral - paper/bone - brushed metal - membranes - evidence artifacts - real depth with semantic purpose

  8. Evidence/governance is part of the design language. Provenance, contradictions, confidence, authority boundaries, receipts, and system states are not backend concepts hidden in logs. The interface is designed to make them visible.

  9. Motion has meaning. Recognition, organization, contradiction, gating, execution, receipt, adaptation. No random floating shit just because WebGL can do it.

  10. Explicit anti-patterns I will now actively reject: - generic glowing AI orb - brain/neural-network imagery - galaxy graphs for decoration - cyberpunk cyan/magenta - glass cards everywhere - meaningless particles - decorative glow - purple-blue SaaS gradients - fake activity - fake confidence - KPI-tile walls as the entire UI - terminal aesthetics leaking into executive/client surfaces - raw JSON as product design

What I'm treating as reference material, not governing law

The Modernist, Industry, and Organic packages are clearly deliberate alternative design experiments. They are useful vocabulary, but they conflict with the master system in places. Likewise, HelmAI is a useful product/interface reference: dense operator cockpit, map, queue, Jarvis interaction, command environment: but I would not infer its green-black treatment is Neuruh master branding. The older Lake Light Neuruh system is more complicated. It has good decisions: warm limestone, lake teal, aged brass, restrained status colors, physical textures: and some of those ideas clearly helped produce the newer master. But the current Master System has moved the governing brand accent from lake teal toward earned brass illumination, with mineral colors becoming product-level accents. So I'm not throwing the Lake-Light work away. I'm treating it as design ancestry + a valid source for specific product personalities, not automatic master-brand law.

Practical hierarchy going forward I'll use this order when something conflicts: Master Design System down to Explicit user-approved/locked direction down to Direction A flagship prototype down to Product-specific design systems down to Older Neuruh systems down to HelmAI / Modernist / Industry / Organic experiments down to Anything newly invented That last part matters. A new AI session does not get to "improve" the brand by randomly introducing a fashionable color system because it likes purple today. I've also preserved this as ongoing context so I can use these design decisions across the work instead of making you re-explain them.

Locked design philosophy You must rebuild the UI to match these principles:
