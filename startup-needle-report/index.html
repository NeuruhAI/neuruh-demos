1. Executive summary

Read this section and you know where the startup stands. Read the rest when you need the receipts.

What Neuruh is. Sovereign AI execution infrastructure, "AI That Executes". Solo founder Jeramie Hicks, based in Benton Harbor, Michigan. The flagship is DeedSonar, a real-estate CRM and lead app, solely his. Under it sits the Lead Recipe OS kernel (30 canonical recipe families), the LANDOS land signal engine (50 signals, SHA-256 provenance receipts), CRUCIBLE hash-chained receipts, Parcel Reality Bench, and a 216-repo GitHub estate. The public proof surface is the Neuruh hub at 113 live sites.

The one-line verdict. This is a pre-revenue startup whose built assets far exceed its revenue. The money path is broken end to end: the flagship is down, the production database is unreachable, the main domain points at a dead deploy, and the only working intake is a scheduling link. Nothing in the Top 5 asks for a new build. Every move is conversion, repair, or closure of something already built.

The Top 5, in one line each.
  1. 1. Convert the cash already on the table. Four paid buyers waiting on ship-or-refund, plus the Kodiak camper sale going cold.
  2. 2. Make one offer actually buyable. A 10-minute Mac backup check decides the data-spine pivot, then DeedSonar redeploys.
  3. 3. Close the first proven outbound loop. Run the pipeline once, end to end, on fresh signals.
  4. 4. File the LLC and confirm a funded card. Standard $50 tier, unlocks SBIR, defense routes, and the A16Z follow-up.
  5. 5. Stop the silent bleed. The chargeback deadline, the full Gmail account, and the dead paid accounts are killing revenue quietly.
