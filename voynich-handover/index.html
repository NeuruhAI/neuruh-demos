Honesty flags, read these first

  • Nothing is translated. No Voynich word has a proven English or Latin meaning.
  • chol, daiin, shol, and otodeeodor are hypotheses, not facts. Treat every gloss of them as a candidate to be tested, never as a definition.
  • No complete grammar, lexicon, language, or English/Latin translation is proven.
  • The manuscript itself is not solved.
  • The breakthrough is the experimental method. The advance is a controlled route from speculation to semantic inference, not a decipherment.
