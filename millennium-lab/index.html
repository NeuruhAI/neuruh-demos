01What the lab is

The atlas stated the six problems and named six computable first moves. The lab is where those moves get executed. Each move is a real program with a real run, a real result file, and a hash-chained receipt, so anyone can re-run it and check it. All six moves ran on November 20, 2026. The first two were calibration runs. The last four are hunts: each names a target, runs at it, and reports what it found, or the bound it established. A move counts only when its receipts exist.

02Riemann: first 1,000 zeros, re-verified

Then, for each located t, an independent mpmath Newton step from 0.5 + i t using zeta and zeta prime, at 30 decimal digits. The Newton step is free to move off the line; the reported real part is where it converges. The two implementations agree: the first zero from PARI matches the mpmath bisection value to 22 digits.

03Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer: one curve family, computed

RUN November 20, 2026. Family: every elliptic curve over Q of conductor at most 20, as enumerated by PARI ellsearch. That is 28 curves in six isogeny classes (conductors 11, 14, 15, 17, 19, 20). There are no curves of conductor 16, and none of conductor 12, 13, or 18. Method: PARI/GP 2.19.1, built from source on this machine, ellanalyticrank at 30 digits of precision. The conductor of every curve was recomputed here with ellglobalred and matches its label. Rank method: analytic rank, the order of vanishing of L(E, s) at s = 1.

Result: every curve has analytic rank 0, and every L(E, 1) is nonzero. That is exactly what Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer predicts for rank 0 curves: rank 0 if and only if L(E, 1) is nonzero. L-values repeat within an isogeny class, because isogenous curves share an L-function. Torsion orders range from 1 to 8. The smallest conductor carrying a rank 1 curve is 37, which is outside this family, so a rank 0 outcome across the family is the expected result, and the run confirms it rather than discovering it.

05Navier-Stokes: the blowup hunt

RUN November 20, 2026. Target: finite-time blowup of unforced 3D Navier-Stokes. Method: pseudo-spectral (Fourier, 2/3 dealiasing) with classical 4th-order Runge-Kutta, periodic cube [0, 2pi]^3. Validation run: Taylor-Green vortex, grid 32^3, viscosity nu = 0.01, dt = 0.01, final time 10.0, 1000 steps, runtime 85.94 seconds. Enstrophy: initial 0.375, peak 0.647, final 0.265. Max vorticity: initial 2.0, peak 4.378, final 2.864. Energy: initial 0.125, final 0.026. Stayed smooth: true. The solver works; the hunt is the deliverable.

The hunt ran three stress cases at viscosity nu = 0.001 to time 10.0, total wall time 10 minutes 16 seconds. Perturbed Taylor-Green (Taylor-Green plus a divergence-free symmetry-breaking mode, amplitude 0.3): enstrophy growth factor 12.00, max vorticity growth factor 7.47, stayed smooth. Anti-parallel vortex tubes (two Gaussian tubes, opposite circulation, core radius 0.5, separation 1.6): growth factor exactly 1.00 in both measures, a genuine negative, the tubes diffused rather than stretching on this timescale. Kida-Pelz-type (high-symmetry field u = sin x cos y cos z, v = cos x sin y cos z, w = -2 cos x cos y sin z): enstrophy growth 14.23 at 32^3 and 18.29 at 48^3, max vorticity growth 8.06 at 32^3 and 7.97 at 48^3, stayed smooth. The 48^3 rerun grew enstrophy more but max vorticity slightly less, which is consistent with resolution changing the small-scale picture without changing the bounded outcome.

Strongest signal: Kida-Pelz at 48^3, enstrophy growth factor 18.29, max vorticity growth factor 7.97, final time 10.0, stayed smooth. No finite-time blowup was observed in any case. Bound established: on grids 32^3 and 48^3 at viscosity 0.001 to time 10, the strongest unforced case reached enstrophy growth factor 18.29 and max vorticity growth factor 8.06 and stayed smooth. Honest limit: a 32^3 or 48^3 grid at nu = 0.001 is under-resolved for a blowup claim in either direction; these runs bound what this grid can see, they do not settle the continuum question. The next hunt step is higher resolution on the Kida-Pelz case and a longer time window.

Background, not a verdict: OpenAI announced on September 8, 2026 a forced finite-time blowup claiming Clay statements (C) and (D), with a Lean formalization, and Clay replied on September 11, 2026 that the problem has apparently been settled while stressing its evaluation is deliberately unhurried. As of the sources read on November 20, 2026, no prize has been awarded, no refereed publication or completed independent review has been confirmed, the two-year Clay clock has therefore not started, the unforced statements (A) and (B) remain open, and the priority dispute with Buckmaster and Alpoege (forced Euler, posted September 7, 2026) is unresolved. The announcement page, the Clay statement, and an explainer quoting the manuscript were read; the full 166-page manuscript was not read line by line, and the Lean formalization was not downloaded, built, or run.

06P versus NP: the discovery hunt on recipe search

RUN November 20, 2026. Target: a structural property of recipe search that points at separation or collapse. The weapon is the complexity object, built from the real repo NeuruhAI/lead-recipe-os (main, updated 2026-10-03). Decision problem RECIPE-COMPOSITION-SEARCH: given a subject timeline T over the signal vocabulary, does there exist a composed recipe, a LogicNode tree over registered signals satisfying the composer validity rules in lead_recipe_os/composer/compose.py and validate.py, whose membership evaluation by lead_recipe_os/signals/graph.py evaluate_logic against T returns SATISFIED? The problem is three-valued (SATISFIED / CONTRADICTED / UNKNOWN) per the constitution in ADR-0001, where UNKNOWN is not 0, not false, and not absent. The registry version, RECIPE-SEARCH, asks the same with the recipe ranging over the 558 registered recipes instead of over composed trees.

Class membership, measured by running the real code: registry search is in P (558 recipes: 58 canonical library, 500 curated; 317 signals, 107 ingredients, 154 sources, 3293 territories; full sweep 0.0718 seconds on full evidence, 0.0093 seconds on an empty timeline; 140 SATISFIED, 11 CONTRADICTED, 407 UNKNOWN on full evidence, because clauses need values, dates, or relations, not mere presence). Bounded-clause composition search for fixed k is in P (5,309,433 candidates at k at most 3 over 317 signals, each checked in O(k) clause evaluations). Composition search is in NP, witnessed by the LogicNode tree plus evaluation trace, checked in linear time. Unknown: whether unbounded composition search is in P, whether it is NP-complete (no reduction given or claimed), and whether the defeater-free monotone fragment admits polynomial-time search.

Hunt findings, both run on the real evaluator. First, membership is not monotone in evidence: a defeater NOT-clause returns SATISFIED on an empty timeline and CONTRADICTED once the defeater signal is present, while a plain 3-clause ALL recipe progressed UNKNOWN, UNKNOWN, UNKNOWN, SATISFIED as signals were added. Second, a sharp phase transition in random composed recipes, 200 trials per cell: match fraction tracks p^r (p=0.5: 0.52 at r=1, 0.115 at r=3, 0.02 at r=5, 0.0 at r=12; p=0.25: 0.0 from r=5; p=0.75: 0.0 at r=20). One cell ran high and is reported as measured: p=0.25, r=1, 0.335 vs predicted 0.25. Hunt verdict: the NP shape lives in composition, not registry lookup; bounded-clause search is polynomial, so hardness must live at unbounded clause counts where matches are exponentially rare; the defeater rule breaks monotonicity and marks the defeater-free fragment as the open attack surface. No separation or collapse is claimed.

Barrier audit, the map of dead ends: relativization does not apply to this object (concrete finite problem, no oracle parameter), and it blocks lifting any finding here to P vs NP by a relativizing argument. Natural proofs applies to the circuit-lower-bound route (the matching predicate is constructive via the linear-time evaluator and dense at low clause counts), and it blocks natural-property circuit lower bounds for composition search, nothing else. Algebrization does not apply (the repo defines no algebraic extension or arithmetization of the evaluator), and it blocks nothing as the object stands. Self-reducibility of the composition decision problem is not established and is recorded as open. The composition-space counts cover required-signal subsets only; any_of groups, negative and absent clauses, temporal windows, sequence, recurrence, and relations multiply the space further and were not enumerated.

07Yang-Mills: the mass gap hunt

RUN November 20, 2026. Target: the mass gap signal on the lattice, extrapolated toward the continuum limit. Method: 8^4 SU(2) Wilson lattice (SU(2) is cheaper than SU(3) with the same confinement physics at this scale), Metropolis with checkerboard even/odd updates, random SU(2) rotation near identity (eps 0.35), seed 20261120, cold start. Thermalization 400 sweeps, measurement 1500 sweeps per beta. Total runtime 338.6 seconds.

Average plaquette: 0.568932 plus or minus 0.000566 at beta 2.2 (acceptance 0.466) and 0.631483 plus or minus 0.000365 at beta 2.4 (acceptance 0.427). Creutz ratios at beta 2.2: chi(2,2) 0.4054 plus or minus 0.0027, chi(2,3) 0.3676 plus or minus 0.0050, chi(3,3) 0.3033 plus or minus 0.0103. At beta 2.4: chi(2,2) 0.2444 plus or minus 0.0018, chi(2,3) 0.1964 plus or minus 0.0028, chi(3,3) 0.1275 plus or minus 0.0033. String tension sigma a^2 from chi(2,2): 0.4054 plus or minus 0.0027 at beta 2.2, 0.2444 plus or minus 0.0018 at beta 2.4.

Gap estimates, three channels, two spacings. 0++ glueball: 1.969 plus or minus 0.159 at beta 2.2, 2.705 plus or minus 0.306 at beta 2.4. 2++ tensor glueball: 4.483 plus or minus 0.867 at beta 2.2, 3.224 plus or minus 0.340 at beta 2.4. Torelon (spatial Polyakov loop correlator): 2.673 plus or minus 0.799 at beta 2.2, 1.739 plus or minus 0.136 at beta 2.4. m over sqrt(sigma) for the 0++ channel: 3.09 at beta 2.2, 5.47 at beta 2.4. Static potential V(1): 0.4980 plus or minus 0.0117 at beta 2.2, 0.3940 plus or minus 0.0067 at beta 2.4.

Extrapolation: two points cannot fix one. The glueball m_eff(0) rises with beta while the cleaner m_eff(1) falls with errors too large to settle the direction, and m over sqrt(sigma) moves substantially between spacings, so no stable continuum value can be read off. The torelon channel falls with beta as expected and stays nonzero at both spacings, which points toward a nonzero continuum gap. This is a hint from two points, not a proof of the continuum mass gap. A real extrapolation needs at least three beta values, larger volumes, and better glueball operators.

Background, not a verdict: the November 10, 2026 independent assessment judges that the OpenAI submission proves a Wightman quantum field theory can be constructed as a scaling limit of Wilson lattice gauge theory for any simple compact gauge group, with a clean Lean rebuild and Comparator checks passed, but it also states the infinite-volume limit is not proven unique and no mass gap is claimed. Nielsen's November 2026 critique argues this does not establish existence in the Clay sense because convergence is subsequential only, the non-triviality test does not exclude generalized free fields, and the contact terms are uncontrolled, with identification as Yang-Mills supported by evidence rather than closed by proof. The Clay two-year clock cannot start because there is no peer-reviewed publication and the mass gap half is entirely open. The 1,147-page informal manuscript is unreleased and was not read; the Lean code was not read line by line.

08Hodge: a machine-checked proof of a proved case

RUN November 20, 2026. Target: a new machine-checked result. Toolchain: Lean 4.13.0 (leanprover/lean4:v4.13.0), mathlib v4.13.0, installed under lean/ with the precompiled cache (5,366 files fetched). Build command lake build completed successfully: 0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 sorries. File: lean/Hodge/Basic.lean.

Proved, all kernel-checked, no sorry. Hodge.hodgeConj_involutive: conjugation on degree 2 cohomology is an involution, axioms [propext]. Hodge.real_vanishing: a real vector in the Hodge decomposition whose (0,2) component vanishes also has vanishing (2,0) component, hence is of type (1,1), proved unconditionally from the definitions, no axioms. Hodge.hodge_class_iff_vanishing: for a real class, being Hodge of type (1,1) is equivalent to (0,2)-vanishing alone, conditional on hreality (integral classes are real), no axioms. Hodge.lefschetz_one_one: every Hodge class of type (1,1) is algebraic, conditional on hsurj (the first Chern class map surjects onto the kernel of H^2(X, Z) to H^{0,2}(X), i.e. the exponential sequence), axioms [propext]. An axiom audit confirms no theorem depends on sorryAx. The general Hodge conjecture is stated as a formal Prop over abstract data (HodgeData), open for p at least 2, and is not claimed proved.

Blocking piece: the unconditional (1,1) case needs the exponential sequence 0 -> Z -> O_X -> O_X^* -> 0 and its long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology, together with the Hodge decomposition identifying H^2(X, O_X) with H^{0,2}(X). Those are the hypotheses hsurj and hreality in the file. Gap list, verified by grepping mathlib v4.13.0 source: singular cohomology groups, Hodge decomposition theorem, cycle class map and Chow groups, first Chern class, Weil and Cartier divisors and the Picard group, the exponential sequence, de Rham cohomology, GAGA, and projective morphism of schemes are all absent. What exists to build on: abstract sheaf cohomology, complex manifolds, smooth morphisms, Proj, and derived functors.

09How to re-run

Everything is in ~/workspace/millennium-lab/. Riemann: ./pari/bin/gp -q < compute/riemann_scan.gp > data/riemann_t.txt (PARI/GP 2.19.1, built from source, installed under pari/ in this folder), then python3 compute/riemann_verify.py (mpmath 1.2.1, system Python 3.12.3). BSD: ./pari/bin/gp -q < compute/bsd_curves.gp, then python3 compute/bsd_curves.py. Navier-Stokes: python3 compute/navier_stokes.py (validation) and python3 compute/navier_stokes_hunt.py (the hunt), numpy 1.26.4. P vs NP: the hunt ran against a read-only fetch of NeuruhAI/lead-recipe-os via the gh.py helper; the fetch was not saved, so a re-run re-fetches the repo and re-applies the definitions in data/p_vs_np.md. Yang-Mills: python3 compute/wilson_lattice.py (numpy only). Hodge: cd lean && ~/.elan/bin/lake build (elan plus the mathlib cache under ~/.cache/mathlib). Receipts: ~/workspace/sovereign-runtime/.venv/bin/python compute/receipts_append.py appends to the live ledger; compute/receipts.py rebuilds the ledger from scratch for runs 1 and 2 only and must not be re-run on the live ledger. Page: python3 compute/build_page.py. The earlier pure-mpmath scan scripts (riemann_zeros.py, riemann_zeros_v2.py) are kept in compute/ as the record of the slow path: the mpmath Z-function scan costs about 10 ms per evaluation at 30 digits in pure Python, which made 108,400 evaluations too slow, so location moved to PARI and mpmath kept only the independent real-part check.
