2. N-Cell/64 header encoder

Header, 8 bytes: routeMask, organMask, lifecycleMask, authorityMask, memoryMask, confidence 0-255, risk 0-255, outputMask

Derived deterministically from the packet. Route comes from a hash of the author agent string. Confidence is eta scaled to 0-255. Grounding and limits

  • SHA-256, canonical JSON, and the receipt chain are copied verbatim from the unified-chain demo on disk. The eta formula is the estate formula, clamped to 0-100.
  • Note on the prefilled eta inputs: with I_q 88, I_r 90, I_v 85, E_wasted 1, C_violations 0 the literal formula gives a raw value of 336600, which clamps to 100, not 78. The page shows the computed value, raw and capped, and does not repeat the 78 figure as a result. The verdict is PASS either way because 100 is above the 70 threshold.
  • All seven agents are deterministic rules over packet fields plus a seeded confidence. They are not models, not people, and not a real review.
  • Simulation only. No backend, no database, no live system, no real repo, no real git, no real CI. Test evidence shown here is in-page checks only, NOT a real CI run.
  • Content hash dedup: the packet content hash is the SHA-256 of the canonical packet JSON, shown in the packet summary after Update packet.
