Video Script 4: The Attention Economy of AI

60-90 second talking-head. Plain spoken language. One idea: your AI is not slow, it is expensive.

[HOOK, first 3 seconds] Your AI is not slow. It is expensive. And nobody is quoting you the real price.

[BEAT 1] Every system task has two costs. There is the compute cost, the one on the invoice. And there is the attention cost: the minutes you spend reading the output, fixing the mistakes, re-prompting the drift, explaining to other people what the system was supposed to do. That second cost is almost always bigger, and nobody quotes it.

[BEAT 2] Here is the pattern everyone lived through. Week one with agents looks miraculous. Month two looks like a second job. The supervision load grows with the volume. That is not leverage. That is a treadmill with better marketing.

[BEAT 3] So try this. For two weeks, log every minute you spend on each system, and divide it by verified outcomes. Not tasks completed. Outcomes someone checked and confirmed. Most "autonomous" setups come back at a thirty to sixty percent attention tax. That is a system with a human-shaped dependency it did not disclose.

[CLOSE] The measure of the system is not how much it does. It is how little of you it needs to do it well.

Word count: ~205 words. At a natural talking pace (~150 wpm), this runs about 80 seconds. Delivery note: pause after the hook line. Let the "nobody is quoting you the real price" land before moving on.
