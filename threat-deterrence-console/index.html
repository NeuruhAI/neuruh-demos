Threat Deterrence Console

Built from the Notebook Codex (The Wave, the Lattice, and the Recipe). One page, six modules, all running at once: the lattice, the signal analyzer, the scam scorer, the bot farm detector, the resolution intake, and the learning ledger.

Honest status: SIMULATION, browser-local. Keyword matching, not a live model. No data leaves this machine. Nothing you enter is saved anywhere except your own browser. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or local emergency first. Reporting rails: Human Trafficking Hotline 1-888-373-7888, NCMEC CyberTipline report.cybertip.org, FBI IC3 ic3.gov, FTC ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

1 Threat Lattice

The page 14 grid, live. Nodes are capture points; the center is the controller. Click a node to read it. Bounce a signal from the center and watch the read come back: interests, incentive, adjacencies, money.

2 Signal Analyzer

Paste a message, an ad, a profile bio, a job offer, anything. The analyzer runs every signal bank against it at once (brute force: every module scores every input) and returns the read: interests, incentive, adjacencies, money, and the recommended action.

3 Scam Scorer

The hook, line, and sinker checklist from the codex. Check what is present. Each item adds weight. Score 0 to 100, with a verdict band and the action for that band.

4 Bot Farm Detector

Two sample sets of 12 accounts. Run correlation: timing clusters, shared phrases, account age. The flag is phantom consensus: agreement that appears across the group but was never in any independent private record. 20 identical agents are not 20 independent opinions.

5 Resolution Intake

The L12 ingredients as a form: known sources, extra info, resources, cause plus effect, stages, psychology, archetypes. Generates a case packet with a real in-page SHA-256 receipt. Browser-local; nothing leaves this machine. Do not put a child's full name or address in this form unless you are handing the packet directly to law enforcement or NCMEC.

6 Learning Ledger

The recursive learning loop, visible. Every analyzer run and scorer verdict with a confirmed outcome adjusts the matched weights by 0.1, clamped 0 to 2, and logs the change with a timestamp. Brute force: every module scores every input, every outcome feeds back, the weights move. The output is carried out completely: detect, score, deter, disrupt, resolve, report, with a receipt at each step.
