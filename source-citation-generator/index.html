Source Citation Generator

Fill in the fields and the citation builds itself, using the exact templates from the Drive source "Source Citation Generator". No data leaves your browser.

Template: [Author if known]. "[Title of Census]," [Year], [Enumeration District], [Sheet/Page Number], [Household/Family Number], [Line Number], [Person of Interest]; digital image, [Website Name] ([URL] : accessed [Access Date]).

Source example: 1910 U.S. census, Suffolk County, Massachusetts, population schedule, Boston Ward 12, enumeration district (ED) 1503, sheet 14A, dwelling 125, family 216, John Smith; digital image, Ancestry.com (https://www.ancestry.com : accessed 15 March 2025).

Template: [Location] [Record Type Office], [Certificate/Volume Number], [Person of Interest], [Event Date]; [Repository or Website], [URL or Physical Location], [Access Date].

Source example: Massachusetts Department of Public Health, Registry of Vital Records and Statistics, death certificate no. 12345 (1915), John Smith; digital image, FamilySearch (https://www.familysearch.org : accessed 15 March 2025).

What this is

Built from the Drive document "Source Citation Generator" (Neuruh Content, modified 2025-04-01). The source gives two templates, census records and vital records, each with one worked example. This page turns both templates into working forms. The examples use John Smith as the sample person of interest, exactly as in the source.

Honest labeling: this is a formatting tool. It does not verify that a record exists, that a URL is correct, or that a citation is complete for any particular publisher. Check the record yourself before relying on the output.
