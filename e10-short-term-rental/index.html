15How it works / What this is not

How it works: confirm the parcel from at least 2 public sources, verify STR legality from zoning, HOA documents, and the ordinance, collect ADR from at least 2 independent sources, enter seasonality in days, price furnishing line by line, enter fixed and variable costs, and let the tool compute blended ADR, annual gross, break-even occupancy, STR Year 1 and stabilized cash flow, MTR cash flow, and LTR fallback cash flow from the exact formulas on the page. The choice in section 10 follows the stated rule. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL hash so someone else can open the same numbers in their own browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not legal, tax, or lending advice, and not a guarantee of occupancy, ADR, rent, or a sale. It does not invent comps, zoning, prices, or ordinance text. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. A high STR cash flow does not mean a deal is good, and a PASS does not mean a property is bad. It means the evidence in this run does not support proceeding as an STR on the stated terms. No offer is quoted without Jeramie.

Honest gaps and limits. This tool does not compute a mortgage payment; PITI is entered as a monthly number. It does not pull live platform data, AirDNA data, or county records; every ADR, occupancy, rent, and legality fact is entered by hand with a source. Seasonality uses the entered days and rates only and does not model events, new supply, or platform algorithm changes. Furnishing amortisation uses a flat 60 month life, which is an assumption, not a tax rule. Cleaning cost assumes turnovers equal booked nights divided by average stay. MTR and LTR costs are simplified and do not include vacancy loss beyond the entered LTR vacancy or MTR occupancy, leasing fees, or eviction cost. Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser.
