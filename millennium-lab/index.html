The Riemann run re-verifies known ground: the first 2,000 zeros were already known to lie on the critical line, and this run checks that fact again with an independent implementation. 01What the lab is

The atlas stated the six problems and named six computable first moves. The lab is where those moves get executed. Each move is a real program with a real run, a real result file, and a hash-chained receipt, so anyone can re-run it and check it. Round 1 ran the two calibration moves and named the four hunts. Round 2 ran all four hunts, plus calibration extensions: BSD to conductor 30 and Riemann to 2,000 zeros. A move counts only when its receipts exist.

The receipt chain is the sovereign runtime receipt format, direct reuse: the production SovereignStore class from ~/workspace/sovereign-runtime, canonical JSON, SHA-256, each receipt carrying the hash of the previous one, GENESIS head. The lab ledger is a separate store (data/lab.db) in the same format. The chain was verified after the store was closed and reopened, which is the restart proof. 02Riemann: first 2,000 zeros, re-verified

RUN November 20, 2026, round 2. Method: PARI/GP 2.19.1 lfunzeros locates every zero with imaginary part in [0, 2560] (C implementation, Riemann-Siegel, argument principle). Then, for each located t, an independent mpmath Newton step from 0.5 + i t using zeta and zeta prime, at 30 decimal digits. The Newton step is free to move off the line; the reported real part is where it converges. The two implementations agree: the first zero from PARI matches the mpmath bisection value to 22 digits.

Result: 2000 zeros checked, first at t = 14.1347, last at t = 2515.2865. Largest deviation of a zero real part from 1/2: 0.0 at 30-digit precision (no zero deviated: every Newton step returned Re = 1/2 exactly at this precision, so the deviation is below 1e-30). Largest |zeta(1/2 + i t)| at a located zero: 6.5408e-26. Count check against the Riemann-von Mangoldt formula at T: expected 1999.38 zeros, found 2,000, so S(T) = 0.620, inside the small range the formula allows. The first 1,000 t values match round 1 exactly. PARI location scan took 907.3 seconds for the upper interval [1420, 2560] (the round 1 scan of [0, 1420] was reused); the mpmath verification pass took 428.91 seconds. The full [0, 2560] scan overflowed the default 8 MB PARI stack, so the interval was split; that workaround is recorded for round 3.

This is a re-verification, not a new record. The first 1013 zeros were already verified on the line by others. This run checks the first 2,000 again, independently, on this machine, with receipts.

04Navier-Stokes: Kida-Pelz at 643, round 2

RUN November 20, 2026. Unforced blowup search, Clay statements (A) and (B) territory, on a small grid. A numerical hunt on a 643 grid cannot prove or disprove a continuum statement, and nothing here claims otherwise. Method: pseudo-spectral, nu 0.001, dt 0.01, t = 10, 1000 steps, same protocol as round 1.

Case 1, Kida-Pelz at 643: enstrophy growth 18.86 (initial 1.125, peak 21.22), max vorticity growth 11.51 (peak 34.52), energy 0.375 to 0.149. Stayed smooth, no blowup observed. Runtime 1516.7 seconds. Resolution trend: enstrophy growth 14.23 at 323, 18.29 at 483, 18.86 at 643. The 48 to 64 step adds only about 3 percent, so growth is saturating with resolution, not running away. Peak vorticity growth is more resolution-sensitive: 7.97 at 483, 11.51 at 643.

Case 2, high-amplitude anti-parallel vortex tubes at 483 (new initial condition): circulation tripled to gamma 6.0, core radius narrowed to 0.4, centre-line displacement doubled to 0.2 sin(y). Vortex stretching is quadratic in vorticity while diffusion is linear, so round 1's flat result at gamma 2.0 may have been an amplitude threshold; this tests localised collision and reconnection, a different stress axis from Kida-Pelz global mode coupling. Enstrophy growth 1.02, max vorticity growth 1.04. Stayed smooth. Runtime 310.7 seconds. Tripling circulation plus a narrower core plus a doubled Crow seed did not activate the collision; the tube null was not an amplitude threshold in this range.

Reading: about 19x finite enstrophy growth that saturates with resolution is a hint, not a regularity proof. The unforced case stays open.

05P versus NP: the defeater-free fragment, round 2

RUN November 20, 2026. Repo NeuruhAI/lead-recipe-os, HEAD 7363dcb1, fetched read-only. Round 1 built the complexity object and left one open attack surface: the defeater-free fragment, where membership is monotone in evidence. Round 2 attacks it.

The fragment, DF-COMP, is the set of recipes buildable by the repo composer whose signal logic contains no clause with defeater=True: a flat ALL over nine node forms (required leaves, ANY groups of leaves, NOT of a leaf, ABSENT, WITHIN_DAYS, SEQUENCE, RECURRENCE, NEAR, ADJACENT), no COUNT_AT_LEAST, CONVERGENCE, CONTRADICTION_FREE, or temporal-change ops, no nesting deeper than ALL over ANY over a leaf, and the recipe must pass validate(). The composer never emits a defeater (0 of 198 randomly built recipes; defeaters exist only in 7 hand-built canonical recipes, 8 clauses total).

Constructive direction, succeeds for the fragment: a node-wise greedy search decides DF-COMP-SEARCH in polynomial time. Nodes evaluate independently and ALL is SATISFIED exactly when every child is, so a SATISFIED recipe exists exactly when an individually SATISFIED node exists that validates. Full evidence: 1331 satisfied nodes of 2599 candidates, witness built, validated, and re-evaluated SATISFIED on attempt 1, in 0.57 seconds. Random p = 0.5 timeline: 857 satisfied of 2,441, witness on attempt 1. Brute-force check: 44 of 44 agreement in 3.21 seconds. Exhaustive 3-node enumeration is estimated at about 567,586 seconds against 0.57 seconds greedy. One validity-aware divergence was found and honored: an empty timeline with resolved relations has 2 SATISFIED relational nodes but NO valid witness (both fail evidence feasibility), so the decision there is NO, not YES.

Hardness direction, stalls structurally: the natural 3-SAT encodings fail both directions of the equivalence, because the evaluator matches trees against the timeline and any decidable literal yields a trivial SATISFIED tree. Encoding A distinguishes SAT from UNSAT under one assignment but the trivial one-leaf tree is SATISFIED on the same timeline, so an UNSAT formula still answers YES; greedy finds nothing on an empty timeline, so a SAT formula still answers NO. Encoding B never consults the formula.

Verdict: the fragment admits an explicit polynomial-time search algorithm, validated against brute force. Nothing here separates or collapses P and NP, and none is claimed. The full-logic question is untouched by this result.

06Yang-Mills: third beta point and a three-point extrapolation, round 2

RUN November 20, 2026. Lattice 84, SU(2), Metropolis checkerboard, eps 0.35, cold start, 400 thermalization plus 1500 measurement sweeps, same protocol as round 1. Beta 2.3, plus a 104 volume check at the same beta. Total runtime 1683.6 seconds. A lattice gap at finite spacing is a measurement, not a proof of the continuum mass gap.

Beta 2.3 on 84: average plaquette 0.602788 plus or minus 0.000484, acceptance 0.445. String tension sigma a2 0.3166 plus or minus 0.0028, between the round 1 values 0.4054 at beta 2.2 and 0.2444 at beta 2.4. Gap estimates: 0++ glueball 2.8292 plus or minus 0.3597, 2++ tensor 3.3770 plus or minus 0.4166, torelon 1.8602 plus or minus 0.2302 (m_eff(1), 12 percent error, the cleanest channel). The torelon sits between 2.673 at beta 2.2 and 1.739 at beta 2.4; the fall from 2.2 to 2.3 is large, 0.81, and from 2.3 to 2.4 is small, 0.12. Static potential V(1) 0.4317 plus or minus 0.0088. The torelon mass is nonzero at all three spacings with clear statistical significance.

Three-point continuum extrapolation, torelon channel, m/sqrt(sigma) against sigma a2: points 4.198 (beta 2.2), 3.306 (beta 2.3), 3.519 (beta 2.4), non-monotonic. Weighted linear fit: continuum intercept 3.473 plus or minus 1.506, slope 0.011 plus or minus 5.486, chi2/dof 0.53/1. What the fit does support: a linear a2 fit can now be performed at all, which two points could not do, and the intercept is nonzero at 2.3 sigma. What it does not support: a continuum claim. The slope error is 500 times the slope value, the intercept error is 43 percent, the beta 2.2 error dominates, sigma comes from chi(2,2) which overestimates the asymptotic tension, the 84 volume leaves finite-volume effects uncontrolled, and the linear form is assumed with only 1 degree of freedom. This is an extrapolation attempt, not a continuum result.

Volume check, 104 at beta 2.3: bulk observables identical within errors (plaquette 0.602805 plus or minus 0.000273, sigma 0.3148 plus or minus 0.0017). The torelon behaves as a flux-tube state should: m_eff(1) 3.7823 plus or minus 2.1275 on 104 against 1.8602 on 84, consistent with mass scaling as sigma times L_s (predicted 3.148 against 2.533). One volume pair at one beta is a check, not a finite-volume extrapolation.

07Hodge: the exponential sequence, round 2

RUN November 20, 2026. Lean 4.13.0 plus mathlib v4.13.0, project under lean/. Round 1 proved Lefschetz (1,1) conditionally on hsurj: the first Chern class map surjects onto the kernel of H2(X, Z) to H0,2(X). In reality that surjectivity is the long exact sequence of the exponential sequence. Round 2 formalizes the tractable piece of that gap.

Piece formalized: the exponential sequence of abelian groups 0 to Z to C to C* to 1, exact at every term, in Hodge/Exponential.lean. The sheaf-level sequence 0 to Z to O_X to O_X^* to 0 was assessed first and is not tractable in mathlib v4.13.0: mathlib has complex manifolds and Ext-based sheaf cohomology, but no sheaf of holomorphic functions O_X to sheafify with. The group-level sequence is the pointwise, stalk-level content of the sheaf sequence, and it is the analytic heart of the (1,1) case.

New theorems, 9 in total: exp is a homomorphism C to C*; ker exp equals 2 pi i Z; every nonzero complex number has a logarithm; the inclusion Z to C is injective; exactness at C; exactness at every term; a bridge theorem restating hsurj as Function.Exact exactness of (c1, projection to H0,2) and deriving algebraicity via round 1 lefschetz_one_one; and a lemma pinning down the role of hreality (a real vector is determined by its (1,1) and (0,2) components) without proving it.

Build: lake build exit 0, 3060/3060 targets, no errors or warnings, 0 sorries in project files. Axiom audit of all 13 theorems (4 round 1 plus 9 new): no sorryAx anywhere; new exponential theorems depend only on standard axioms [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]. Still blocked: the sheaf O_X and its units, the long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology with the connecting homomorphism identified with c1, the Dolbeault identification of H2(X, O_X) with H0,2(X), and the Hodge decomposition itself, which would discharge hreality. The unconditional (1,1) case remains conditional.
