1. What this document is, and what it is not

What it is. The deep brand master for Neuruh. It gathers the two governing visual authorities, the Master Design System v1.0 and the Lake Michigan Modern canon, and records them at full depth: tokens with hex values where the sources give them, usage rules per token, typography roles, material language, motion rules, semantic state treatments, anti-patterns, the hero sequence, the voice lines the record actually contains, the logo and lockup files on record, the Notion brand records, and the one open palette ruling with both sides stated.

What it is not. It is not a replacement for the Master Design System source file, which has not been recovered. It is not a summary of unread Drive documents. It is not a new palette, a new logo, or a new lockup. Nothing in it invents a hex value, a type size, a lockup rule, or a voice line that no source states. Where a value is not on record, the entry says UNKNOWN and gives the reason.

2. Brand architecture: one foundation, many faces

The brand architecture is one line from the Design Direction Summary filing (ChatGPT conversation, Aug 25, 2026): "Share the foundation. Never share the face." Products share infrastructure, typography roles, evidence semantics, governance semantics, accessibility, and components. They do not all look like reskinned Neuruh.com.

The flagship visual thesis is Biological-Architectural: engineered tissue, membranes, evidence channels, a hard crystalline governance core, and receipts returning through the system. That gives Neuruh a real spatial metaphor instead of generic AI graphics. The thesis is recorded in the Design Direction Summary filing, which names the Master Design System ZIP as the strongest source in the estate: it identifies itself as the source of truth for the flagship work, products, evidence and trust, and Blackbox diagnostics.

What the thesis forbids. A new session does not get to improve the brand by introducing a fashionable color system. The hierarchy in Section 3, Figure 2, is the guardrail: the Master Design System wins every conflict, and anything newly invented sits at the bottom of the order.

Product personalities. Product verticals may introduce controlled mineral cyan, indigo, violet, or clay accents, but only if justified. The filing records the older Lake Light system as design ancestry, not law: warm limestone, lake teal, aged brass, restrained status colors, physical textures. The current Master System moved the governing brand accent from lake teal toward earned brass illumination, with mineral colors becoming product-level accents. HelmAI is a useful product and interface reference (dense operator cockpit, map, queue, command environment), but its green-black treatment is not Neuruh master branding. The Modernist, Industry, and Organic packages are deliberate alternative design experiments: useful vocabulary, but they conflict with the master system in places and do not govern.

The two-spine rule. Dark is not "the Neuruh look." Obsidian is depth, authority, and diagnostics. Bone is evidence, explanation, and editorial surfaces. The split exists so everything does not become another black hacker dashboard. A surface that over-indexes on all-dark everything is failing the system; the Lead Recipe OS rebuild prompt names exactly that failure and requires Bone introduced strategically for evidence panels, explanatory panels, receipts, selected detail panes, provenance drawers, and review modules.

3.3 Material language

Surfaces must feel physical, architectural, mineral, and engineered, not glossy generic SaaS. The filing lists the required feel: machined plates, mineral, paper and bone, brushed metal, membranes, evidence artifacts, real depth with semantic purpose. Required adjectives: machined, mineral, restrained, crisp, layered, engineered, tactile without being skeuomorphic, architectural rather than glossy, trustworthy rather than flashy.

Evidence and governance are part of the design language. Provenance, contradictions, confidence, authority boundaries, receipts, and system states are not backend concepts hidden in logs. The interface is designed to make them visible. State must not be expressed only by tiny pills and wall-of-text logs; it needs hierarchy.

3.5 Motion: meaning only

Motion has meaning: recognition, organization, contradiction, gating, execution, receipt, adaptation. No random floating decoration because WebGL can do it. Any motion must reveal evidence, trace causality, expand provenance, show gated execution, distinguish blocked from granted, focus attention, or confirm receipt generation. No decorative motion.

3.6 Anti-patterns: actively rejected

  • Generic glowing AI orb; brain or neural-network imagery; galaxy graphs for decoration
  • Cyberpunk cyan and magenta; purple-blue SaaS gradients; decorative glow; meaningless particles
  • Glass cards everywhere; excessive blur; glossy generic SaaS surfaces
  • Fake activity; fake confidence; KPI-tile walls as the entire UI
  • Terminal aesthetics leaking into executive or client surfaces; raw JSON as product design
  • Neon gradients; floating particles; meaningless glassmorphism; decorative AI cliches; ornamental 3D inside operator UI
  • Fake charts; meaningless trend lines; decorative intelligence motifs

Note on scope: the anti-pattern list forbids purple-blue SaaS gradients and AI gradients generally. That is the Master Design System side of the open palette ruling in Section 9. The Canva playbook side is stated there, not settled here.

4.2 The hero: a 6-second signal-to-receipt loop

The hero is a 4 to 6 second loop, stated as 6 seconds in the locked canon: radar sweep over the Lake Michigan and Michiana map, parcel lights up, data cards pop, then a DeedSonar screenshot and an Opportunity Intelligence Brief. Use that name publicly, not "codex." The sequence, as locked in the filing:

  1. Dark terrain, radar sweep over Michiana.
  2. Signal pulse, a parcel lights up.
  3. Evidence pulled, evidence cards snap in.
  4. Grounded in LANDOS, domain intelligence normalizes the evidence.
  5. Recipes interpret, the recipe engine ranks the play.
  6. Governance gates, the gate flashes allow, escalate, or block.
  7. DeedSonar console, the operator approves and the action card shows.
  8. Execution, then a receipt writes back.
  9. Zoom-out: the same loop applies to other markets. One machine, not a buffet.

Production rules for the hero. Blender plus Three.js, restrained, over realistic Lake Michigan terrain. Glass panels over the terrain, radar sweep, signal pulse, evidence cards, governance gate, then a real screenshot. No fakes. Real product shots where possible, no generic AI art, no purple glows. Copy stays short. If it starts looking like generic AI confetti, kill it fast. The handoff line recorded in the filing is: "Build a Lake Michigan command center aesthetic."

Positioning rule from the filing. If the site only shows a real estate radar, the market will pigeonhole Neuruh. Position Neuruh as the execution intelligence layer, the operating layer for overlooked opportunity. Use the real estate flow only as the most legible demo, then show visually that the same loop works across other verticals: land, resale, services, liquidity. Not a list of industries, but a repeating pattern. DeedSonar is the flagship proof case of something larger.

Proof rules. Show real examples, not hallucinations or hypotheticals. Clean interface around Lake Michigan, scanning the Michiana area. Use real screenshots to anchor the site. No fake hologram command center. No purple AI sludge. Evidence must be real: the filing requires cutaways to screenshots of DeedSonar and things already built, so the claims are not hypothetical.

9. Open ruling: the brand palette, Canva playbook vs Master Design System

Status: UNDECIDED. Decider: Jeramie. This section presents both sides in full and settles nothing. Until Jeramie rules, the standing authority is the Master Design System v1.0 under the Figure 2 hierarchy: brass single accent, Obsidian and Bone spines, Archivo, Inter, JetBrains Mono. This document does not invent a Canva palette and does not treat the ruling as settled.

The question. Which palette authority governs Neuruh surfaces: a Canva playbook, or the Master Design System v1.0?

9.1 Side A: Master Design System v1.0

  • Palette: Bone, Paper, Ink, and Brass. Bone #FAF9F6 is the principal warm light, Obsidian #0B0D12 is the canonical dark, Brass #DDBA6A / #B08535 is the single accent. Ink is the text color family on Bone surfaces; no Ink hex value is stated in the filings, so no value is given here.
  • Gradients: forbidden. The anti-pattern list rejects purple-blue SaaS gradients, AI gradients, neon gradients, and decorative glow. Brass is semantic, not decorative.
  • Evidence for this side: the Design Direction Summary filing, Aug 25, 2026, records the Master Design System ZIP as the strongest source in the estate and the source of truth for flagship work, products, evidence and trust, and Blackbox diagnostics. The hierarchy in Figure 2 puts it first. Doc 5, Section 6, Ruling 1, records the same standing authority.
  • What it governs: every Neuruh surface, under the Figure 2 hierarchy, until Jeramie rules otherwise.

9.2 Side B: Canva playbook

  • Palette: Core Midnight, Tech Cobalt, and Signal Cyan, as named in the brief for this document. No hex values for any of the three names were supplied with the brief, and no hex values are stated in any source read for this document, so all three hex values are UNKNOWN and none are invented here.

10. Applying the brand: the rebuild order and acceptance bar

The rebuild order. The Lead Recipe OS rebuild prompt from the Design Direction Summary filing gives the execution order for realigning any surface to the Master Design System. It is a design custody order, not a suggestion:

  1. Break it. Audit the current UI: visual system, component inventory, token usage, hierarchy failures, accessibility, density, state semantics, spacing, typography, missing seams.
  2. Fix it. Refactor layout, component hierarchy, spacing, typography, and token usage. Create theme tokens and semantic state components.
  3. Polish it. Introduce Bone strategically, make receipts and evidence feel premium, keep operator density without ugliness.
  4. Retest it. Visual correctness, functional correctness, accessibility basics, responsive stability, regression, theme integrity, route integrity, data rendering integrity.
  5. Connect missed seams. Stale tokens, one-off old cards, inconsistent badge styles, inconsistent spacing, dead hover states, mismatched typography, orphaned routes.
  6. Verify live. Verify the rebuilt screens in the running app. Do not claim done without visual confirmation.
  7. Produce final receipt. Files changed, components changed, tokens added or modified, routes touched, tests run, results, unresolved issues, screenshots before and after, exact seams connected, exact live verification steps.

The acceptance bar. The work is not complete unless all of the following are true, per the rebuild prompt: the new UI clearly aligns with the Master Design System; the screen no longer reads like a generic dark dashboard; the product retains dense operator utility; evidence, governance, authority, predicates, and receipts are visually legible; Obsidian and Bone both appear meaningfully; brass is used sparingly and semantically; typography roles are correct; semantic state treatments are consistent; the key screens are visually more premium, more legible, and more trustworthy; no major route or feature regressions were introduced; all tests possible in the environment were run and reported; final screenshots and implementation receipt are produced.

What a builder must feed the rebuild. The filing lists the feed package: the Master Design System ZIP, the Direction A prototype ZIP, the Three World Directions ZIP, HelmAI ZIP as a density and operator-flow reference only (not brand law), the current screenshots, the target repo path, current theme and token files, and a request to produce audit, implementation, verification, and final receipt. Two of those inputs are not recoverable from this VM (the Master Design System ZIP and the Direction A ZIP), which is why Gap 1 in Section 11 is the first blocker for any rebuild that claims full compliance.

What a builder must not do. Do not invent random brand colors; do not make everything glossy or neon; do not turn an operator product into a consumer SaaS dashboard; do not remove dense operational information; do not bury evidence; do not collapse important system states; do not use decorative 3D or AI tropes; do not skip testing; do not skip live verification; do not claim completion without a receipt.
