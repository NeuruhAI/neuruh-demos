How It Works

Three steps, in plain language.

  1. Reach out. Tell us about your parcel - where it is, roughly how big, and anything you know about access or back taxes. Phone or email today; a short web form arrives in Phase 1.
  2. Get an offer. We research the parcel and send a written offer, with the reasoning behind the number. If we are not the right buyer, we say so quickly.
  3. Close. A title company handles the paperwork and the funds. No fees or commissions are charged to you.
