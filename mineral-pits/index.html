1 · How the pattern works — placer concentration

Water runs the same algorithm on every rock on Earth. Chemical weathering kills feldspar and mica first — they become the clay layer. Only resistates survive transport: quartz, chert/flint, garnet, zircon, tourmaline, rutile (the ZTR index). Water sorts the survivors by settling velocity, a function of density and size. Clay is the false bedrock: heavies work down through loose gravel until they hit the impermeable clay lens and stop. Chert and flint nodules are lag survivors from limestone that dissolved away around them.

Figure 1 — The trap stratigraphy. Same framework everywhere; only the pigment load (source rock) changes. Southwest Michigan is glacial outwash: pre-sorted sediment, re-sorted by every creek cut.

Thorium = NORM licensing, and it collides with the ITAR/EAR posture on edge-agent. The scorer weights monazite/REE at 0.0 by design.

5 · Honest limits + the open decision

Limits

MRDS ceased systematic updates in 2011 — it is an occurrence archive, not a permit roll. Active operator/contact data comes from MI EGLE and Indiana DNR; that join is the next build, structurally identical to the parcel × probate join. Proximity is not grade. Nothing gets committed without a heavy-liquid separation or Franz run on a real sample.

Monetization, in order of speed

Operators ("what's in my pit" as a paid report — 1,110 active producers in MI/IN alone). Abrasive buyers — garnet feedstock sourcing; live market, boring, pays. Federal — critical-minerals mapping is a funded priority, and the estate already has a defense-adjacent channel (DIU, X-FORCE AQUA); a domestic critical-minerals provenance database is a grant target, not a customer pitch.

Open decision — A / B / C (unanswered as of 2026-11-10)

A — Prove the mineral thesis: sample the top 3 Marquette pits, run separation, get real grades. Cheap. Kills or confirms Plays 1/2/4 permanently. B — Build the data layer for real: MRDS × MI EGLE permits × IN DNR × parcel data → the scored, contactable database. Zero regulatory exposure. C — Both; B funds A. The transcript's read is B: the minerals in reach are dilute; the information about them is not.

Cross-domain note

LandOS running parcels through its signal model is hydraulic sorting; the qualification threshold is the clay contact where the heavies accumulate.
