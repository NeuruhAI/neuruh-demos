Ingredient families A to K

An ingredient is not simply "a field." An ingredient is a reusable intelligence capability. Every ingredient must declare: id, name, family, description, inputs, outputs, prerequisites, source, and more. The registry must be substantially larger than the original taxonomy. Click a family to read what it covers.

A. Source ingredients

County assessor, parcel GIS, tax records, probate, court, code enforcement, permit, recorder, auction, estate, and more. These are the adapters that bring raw public records into the canonical model.

B. Observation ingredients

Sale, listing, price change, tax event, probate event, death event, estate event, auction, permit, code violation, and more. Observations are what happened, recorded as events the system can reason over.

C. Identity ingredients

Person resolution, parcel resolution, address resolution, entity resolution, company resolution, estate resolution, and more. Identity answers the question: are these records about the same person, parcel, or entity? Never silently merge uncertain identities.

D. Enrichment ingredients

Owner tenure, structure age, equity estimate, debt estimate, market value, replacement cost, rent estimate, and more. Enrichment turns a bare record into a usable fact about the object.

E. Derivation ingredients

Long-hold, high-equity, free-and-clear candidate, under-improved, over-improved, distress, succession, transition, and more. Derivations are computed conditions, kept distinct from observed facts.

F. Reasoning ingredients

Comparables, valuation, scenario model, risk model, probability model, expected-value model, information-gain, and more. Reasoning ingredients model what a fact means, not just what it is.

G. Contact ingredients

Contact discovery, contact verification, relationship discovery, authority verification, business relations, and more. Contactability is first-class: finding the right person through the right authority matters as much as finding the property.

H. Economic ingredients

ARV, equity, margin, spread, yield, cap rate, cash-on-cash, IRR, replacement cost, land residual, development, and more. Economics are explicit, never buried inside one opaque score.

I. Temporal ingredients

Age, tenure, duration, recency, velocity, frequency, seasonality, sequence, time since event, time to event, event, and more. Time is a first-class dimension: how long, how recently, how often, in what order.

J. Geospatial ingredients

Distance, adjacency, corridor, frontage, water proximity, road access, parcel shape, assemblage adjacency, geospatial, and more. Place is a first-class dimension: where, next to what, along which corridor.

K. Physical-world ingredients

The major expansion. Estate-sale company detected; auctioneer detected; cleanout activity; moving activity; contractor activity, and more. These become legitimate intelligence inputs. They do not automatically become conclusions. Physical-world signals can reveal transitions before conventional real-estate sources do.

Recipe maturity: click a stage

Every recipe gets a maturity state. The 30 existing recipes must each receive this status. The 500 library must never pretend every recipe is production-ready.

IDEA

A named idea for a strategy. No contract yet, no evidence attached. Ideas are recorded, not run.

PROPOSED

A written recipe proposal with a contract draft. Generated recipes begin here and cannot silently leave it.

SPECIFIED

The contract is complete: ingredients, signals, sources, constraints, economics, evidence requirements, and scoring are all stated.

SIMULATED

The recipe has been simulated against available data to check that it executes, that its sources are reachable, and that its logic holds.

BACKTESTED

Where historical data exists, the recipe has been run against it and its results compared with known outcomes.

SHADOW

The recipe runs against live conditions without becoming customer-visible production. Shadow execution captures results, signals, scores, and cost for qualification.

COUNTY_VALIDATED

The recipe has been validated in a real county with real sources, identity joins, historical depth, and freshness checked.

PRODUCTION

Approved and running for operators. Promotion requires governed approval; production status is earned, not assumed.

DEGRADED

A production recipe whose sources, coverage, or performance have decayed. It stays visible with its degraded state shown, not silently dropped.

RETIRED

Withdrawn from use. Lineage is preserved: the recipe, its versions, and its history remain in the registry.

Next-best-evidence engine

Every incomplete candidate asks: what piece of information would most improve the decision? Possible answers: probate lookup, title lookup, tax history, mortgage release, listing status, owner contact, zoning confirmation. Prioritized by expected information gain, cost, latency, availability, reliability, and decision impact.

0Executive ruling

The system is not a lead scraper, not a property-search application, not a collection of 30 saved filters, not a CRM, not DeedSonar, not LandOS, not FSP. It is a universal opportunity compilation engine. The product has two fundamental modes: Mode A prebuilt recipes, Mode B composable and generated recipes. The first provides speed. The second provides the moat.

1The 30 to 500 to Infinite model

The 30 are the prebuilt menu. The 500+ are the curated library, deliberately authored across verticals. Infinite composition generates candidate recipes from ingredients, signals, relationships, territories, time windows, economic objectives, and strategies, gated by schema validation, ingredient compatibility, source feasibility, evidence feasibility, and territory compatibility. The three-layer recipe system: Layer 1 canonical (human-approved), Layer 2 derived (recombined approved ingredients), Layer 3 experimental (PROPOSED to SIMULATED to BACKTESTED to SHADOW to QUALIFIED to APPROVED to PRODUCTION).

5The ingredient is the real moat

An ingredient is not simply "a field." An ingredient is a reusable intelligence capability. Every ingredient must declare: id, name, family, description, inputs, outputs, prerequisites, source, and more. The filing truncates the full field list here; the missing entries are not reconstructed.

6Ingredient families A to K

Source, Observation, Identity, Enrichment, Derivation, Reasoning, Contact, Economic, Temporal, Geospatial, Physical-world. See Figure 3 for the full family explorer.

7Donor tools are ingredient factories

Existing systems such as DirtHunter, StormStack, House Agent, Estate Sale Prediction, Hidden Equity Extractor, TitleTrace, Probate Miner, and more must be decomposed. A donor tool can produce signals, sources, parsers, normalizers, resolvers, enrichers, scorers, economic models, strategies, and query operators. The donor tool itself is preserved as lineage. Its reusable components become canonical ingredients. Example: Hidden Equity Extractor might yield estimated_equity, debt_age, and mortgage_release_gap ingredients. This is how the old work becomes useful without destroying its provenance.

8The donor lineage graph

Every ingredient should know where it came from. DirtHunter branches into parcel acquisition, rural land signals, acreage filters, and access signals. Nothing is lost. Nothing gets falsely promoted. Everything becomes composable.

9Signals are not ingredients

An ingredient is a capability. A signal is an observation or derived condition. Example: the probate source adapter (ingredient) produces the signal probate_opened = TRUE. The county assessment adapter produces structure_age = 74. The estate-sale detector produces estate_sale_event = TRUE. Then the recipe consumes the signals.

10Signal graph

Every signal should carry: id, subject_id, object_id, type (FACT, EVENT, DERIVED, or PREDICTIVE), and value. The existing Signal Ledger work already establishes much of this structure. The filing truncates the full field list here.

11Signal fusion

One signal should rarely determine the final opportunity. The engine should support single signal, signal pair, signal cluster, signal sequence, signal convergence, signal contradiction, and signal fusion. Example: old structure + long tenure + estate sale + no active listing + high equity + probate is materially different from old structure alone. The engine records the causal and evidentiary graph instead of collapsing everything into one opaque score.

12Linear signals

Signals where magnitude matters continuously: owner tenure of 3, 8, 17, or 42 years; equity of 20%, 45%, or 80%; distance of 0.2, 1.5, or 8 miles; tax delinquency duration. These should not be forced into TRUE or FALSE. They need continuous value, normalization, thresholds, curves, decay, boosts, and penalties.

13Temporal signals

More than "event exists." The engine must recognize event age, event sequence, event frequency, event acceleration, event clustering, event recency, and event decay. Example: tax delinquency to probate to estate sale to listing is different from estate sale to no listing. The sequence itself becomes an ingredient.

14Relational signals

Signals can exist between objects: property adjacent to property; owner owns multiple parcels; estate owns multiple properties; auction company related to property, and more. The graph should support ENTITY, PROPERTY, PERSON, COMPANY, EVENT, LOCATION, and ASSET with typed relationships.

15Negative signals

Explicit negative evidence: active listing; recent arm's-length sale; recent refinance; owner recently updated property; recent major work, and more. Negative evidence should reduce or block recipe membership where appropriate.

16Absence is also data, but only when testable

The system must distinguish NO RECORD FOUND from RECORD DOES NOT EXIST and SOURCE WAS NOT SEARCHED and SOURCE FAILED and SOURCE DOES NOT COVER COUNTY. These are separate states. Never convert source failure into a false negative.

17Predictive ingredients

Predictions must remain distinct from observed facts. Example: FACT owner tenure = 31 years; FACT property built = 1958; FACT estate sale event = TRUE; FACT probate opened. The model never gets to rewrite the facts.

18JGI / AGI layer

JGI and AGI-style reasoning should operate as strategy synthesis and gap resolution, not as an unbounded hallucination engine. It can discover candidate ingredients, identify missing evidence, generate recipe variants, and detect hidden relationships. But all generated artifacts enter PROPOSED before becoming authoritative.

19Autonomous county resolution

One of the highest-value capabilities. Suppose Recipe X worked in County A. County B has the same canonical concept but a different field name, different source, different endpoint, and different document format. The autonomous system should detect the source mismatch, identify the missing canonical field, and search the approved source registry. The recipe itself does not change. The adapter changes.

20Source adapter contract

Every external tool, API, or source must declare: source_id, provider, endpoint, authentication, rate_limit, cost, coverage, rights, schema, version, freshness. If an external API is required, the recipe explicitly declares it. There is never a hidden dependency.

21External tool execution plan

A recipe run produces a plan covering prerequisites, credentials, source availability, source health, and territory. If a provider is unavailable, the result is SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, not 0 RESULTS.

22Query text box

The natural-language search box becomes a first-class compiler. It parses intent, checks available ingredients, and compiles the best runnable plan. See Figure 4.

23The text box must explain itself

It should show its interpretation back to the operator: what it is finding, the geography, and the required signals. This prevents silent interpretation errors.

24Search UI

Natural language, structured filters, presets, and an advanced recipe builder with WHEN, AND, OR, NOT, BOOST, PENALIZE, REQUIRE, EXCLUDE. See Figure 4.

27LandOS

LandOS becomes the real-estate data and spatial execution substrate, not a competing product. Its responsibilities include ingest, normalize, parcel identity, spatial joins, county adapters, property facts, land facts, and geospatial work. The Signal-Opportunity Core consumes those capabilities. LandOS can also consume Core recipes where appropriate. No duplicated truth.

40Recipe differentials

If two recipes are nearly identical, the system should know. Example: Recipe A, Long Hold High Equity; Recipe B, Long Hold High Equity + Estate Signal. The system displays: B adds estate_sale, probate, and ownership_transition; B removes pure equity-only qualification. This makes the library manageable even at 500+.

41Recipe graph

Recipes themselves become nodes. Recipe A is derived from B, uses ingredient C, improves D, overlaps E, and conflicts with another recipe. This prevents 500 recipes from becoming an unsearchable pile.

42Ingredient graph

Same for ingredients. The estate_sale_event ingredient supports Estate Sale to Property and Estate + Tax recipes, and more. This tells us what an ingredient is actually worth.

43Ingredient economics

Every ingredient should have acquisition cost, API cost, compute cost, latency, coverage, reliability, maintenance cost, and legal and data-rights cost. This enables recipe optimization. The source gives an example: Recipe A, 12 ingredients, $4.20 per run, 94% coverage; Recipe B, 7 ingredients, $0.81 per run, 89% coverage. Those figures are the source's example, not measured costs. The operator can choose the operating mode.

45Search operating modes

46Autonomous discovery

The system can be asked to find opportunities nobody has thought of. It should inspect installed ingredients, historical outcomes, and current signal clusters, then present new recipe proposals with evidence.

47Cross-vertical transfer

A major moat. If a signal pattern works in real estate, long tenure plus transition event plus asset age, the engine can ask whether the same abstract pattern exists elsewhere. Automotive: long ownership plus vehicle age plus estate event. Equipment: long ownership plus equipment age plus business closure. Business: long operation plus owner transition plus property or control transition. The system learns structural patterns, not just domain labels.

48Lineage preservation

Every generated thing must know what created it, what sources it, what donor system contributed, what recipe generated it, and what version it is. This is the antidote to AI spaghetti.
