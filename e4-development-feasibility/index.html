02Subject site

Address and parcel must be verified against at least 2 public sources before any number is used. Unverified means HOLD. Zoning and entitlement status come from the county or municipality record, never from a seller claim alone.

03Buildable units

Buildable units are the smaller of proposed units and the maximum the zoning record allows. Density is buildable units divided by site acres. If zoning caps the count below the proposal, the proposal is cut, not the cap.

Enter proposed and maximum units.

04Builder comps

Minimum 1 usable builder comp. A builder comp is a comparable new-build or recent builder sale, not a resale comp. Every comp needs a price, a sale date, and a full source URL. No builder comp is a kill condition: the deal is PASSed, not estimated.

05Revenue

Average sale price per unit must be supported by the builder comps in section 04. Gross revenue is buildable units times average sale price. Land asking price is entered here so profit after land can be shown; it is never used to compute residual land value.

Working, revenue: enter buildable units and average sale price to see the arithmetic.

06Hard costs

Hard costs are construction and site work. Enter a per-unit hard cost from a builder bid or a documented local cost basis, plus site work as a lump sum. Contingency is a percent of the hard subtotal. Low and High scenarios scale the hard total by 0.90 and 1.10. Replace every estimate with a bid as soon as you have one. Required hard-cost buckets on every scope: foundation, framing, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, kitchen, baths, flooring, exterior/paint, site/drive, landscaping. A scope with an empty bucket is incomplete.

Working, hard costs: enter buildable units and a per-unit hard cost to see the arithmetic.

07Soft costs

Soft costs are everything that is not bricks, lumber, and site work. Enter each line as dollars. Financing and carrying is the total interest and carry over the whole timeline, not a monthly figure.

Working, soft costs: enter soft cost lines to see the arithmetic.

08Timeline

Enter each phase in months. Entitlement timeline must be knowable: if it is unknown, select Unknown and the kill-switch fires in section 16. Monthly carrying is total financing and carrying divided by total months.

09Entitlement risk

Check every condition that applies. These are risk flags, not kills by themselves. The kills are listed in section 16: negative residual on Base costs, unknowable entitlement timeline, no builder comp.

14How it works / What this is not / Sources

Read this before relying on any number on this page.

How it works: confirm the address and parcel from at least 2 public sources, enter proposed and maximum units, at least 1 builder comp with a source URL, an average sale price per unit, hard and soft costs, a timeline, and a target margin. The tool computes buildable units as the smaller of proposed and maximum, gross revenue, hard costs with contingency, soft costs, total cost before land, margin before land, and residual land value as gross revenue minus hard costs minus soft costs minus required profit. Low and High scenarios scale the hard total by 0.90 and 1.10. Gates fail closed. A kill means PASS. The receipt records what was entered, what passed, and what was N/A. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a valuation, not a survey, not a feasibility study by a licensed professional, not legal, tax, or lending advice, and not a guarantee of value, cost, timeline, entitlement, or a sale. It does not verify zoning, title, soils, floodplain, wetland, access, or infrastructure capacity; those come from county, municipal, and professional records you enter. It does not pull live comps, costs, or rates. Hard-cost figures are estimates until a builder bid replaces them. A positive residual does not mean a deal is good, and a PASS does not mean a site is bad. It means the evidence in this run does not support proceeding on the stated strategy. It presents no recommendation to buy or sell.

15Honest gaps and limits

What this engine does not do, stated plainly.

  • No live data connection. Sale prices, costs, and timelines are entered by hand and are only as good as their sources.
  • No zoning, title, soils, floodplain, wetland, access, or infrastructure verification. Those come from county, municipal, and professional records you enter.
  • No live comps, costs, or rates. Hard-cost figures are estimates until a builder bid replaces them.
  • Low and High scenarios scale only hard costs, not revenue, soft costs, or timeline.
  • It does not model phased construction, draw schedules, lender terms, tax, or exit friction beyond the lines entered.
  • Residual land value is a modeled estimate of what the land could support at the stated costs and margin. It is not a land value, not an offer price, and not a price a seller will accept.
  • Margin is before tax and before reserves timing. It is not a tax or investment return claim.
  • Receipts live in this browser session only. Closing the page clears the chain. Export the receipt JSON for the file copy.
  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. The page was tested with Node tests plus code inspection only.
