Estate Sale Probability Scorer

Score a probate estate's likelihood of a sale, its timeline, and its disposition. Built from the source doc's own prediction variables. Browser simulation, no real probate data.

Simulation only

Source: Google Doc "Estate Sale Prediction System - Neuruh - Real Estate - Master", Drive 2025-06-21. All market and revenue figures in the source are source claims, not verified facts, and none are used here. Scoring weights are transparent and adjustable. No owner or heir PII is collected. No heir contact is offered. Automated heir contact in the source's Phase 2 is gated by the founder-authorization boundary.

Score an estate

Weights: executor 20, heir proximity 20, condition/value 20, liquidity 20, market 10, complexity 10. Total 100. The property type selector is recorded for the file, per the source, and carries no weight in this version because the source gives no weight for it.
