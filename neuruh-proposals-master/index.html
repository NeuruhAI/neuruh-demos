6. Signal Partner Program and Liquidity on Demand

Concept CONCEPT

What it is. Turn the gig economy into a signal economy. Service businesses that arrive first at a change in the physical world, tree services, roofers, plumbers, junk haulers, estate cleanout operators, movers, contractors, become distributed opportunity sensors, or field originators. They submit an address, event type, photos, and permission. Neuruh enriches, scores, and routes the opportunity. The system is not a lead-generation business, a wholesaling business, an AI-services business, or a lending business. It is a real-time opportunity origination and liquidity-routing infrastructure concept.

Source: verbatim filing of a 17-turn ChatGPT conversation, Aug 22-23, 2026, at ~/workspace/drive-pull/build-signal-partner-program.md. Interactive page, live at https://neuruhai.github.io/neuruh-demos/build-signal-partner-program/ (jsdom 125/125, STATIC-WORKING only). Dollar figures in the source conversation are that conversation's own illustrative figures, preserved verbatim; they are not verified amounts.

6.1 The three layers

  1. Layer One: the signal economy. Service partners as field originators. They keep their core service revenue and their customer trust relationship; Neuruh does the backend work: enrichment, scoring, routing, packaging. Some partners can become co-originators, doing additional legwork and participating in the resulting opportunity.
  2. Layer Two: personal asset liquidity. Asset-rich, cash-poor owners hold fragmented value, cards, collectibles, gold, vehicles, equipment, estate contents, that banks will not underwrite quickly. The product is an Asset Liquidity Router: value it, route it, find the liquidity. The key line from the source: "You don't need to be a lender. You need to be a router." Valuation alone is not the business; liquidity is. Each asset carries multiple values, including immediate cash value and 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day values, a liquidity curve.
  3. Layer Three: transition liquidity. The largest opportunity as framed in the source. People in transition, moving, downsizing, aging, entering assisted living, cleaning out an estate, closing a business, retiring, hold substantial assets but lack liquid capital to manage the transition. The system underwrites the asset pool and its liquidation pathways, not the consumer.

6.2 Two capital asks

As framed in the source, the two asks are kept separate. Platform capital funds the technology and operating company: AI infrastructure, engineering, partner acquisition, operations, legal structure, compliance, sales, data, product development, market expansion. Transaction liquidity capital funds advances, inventory acquisition, bridge capital, asset purchases, deposits, short-duration working capital, warehouse facilities, specific SPVs, and asset-backed execution. The capital stack concept runs from equity capital, to a committed transaction facility, to asset-specific SPVs, to buyer commitments, to strategic capital, to syndicated or marketplace liquidity. No amounts are stated for any of these; none are groundable from the source.

6.3 Farm Continuity Liquidity and Heirloom Asset Recovery

Farm Continuity Liquidity: aging farmers with no clear successor, significant land and equipment value, limited liquid capital. The opportunity is liquidity, succession intelligence, transition support, and alternative ownership or operating structures that preserve productive American agricultural assets. Possible structures to investigate include sale-leaseback, partial land acquisition, long-term lease, operating partnership, farm-management transition, and farmer-to-younger-farmer transfer. Significant specialized legal, tax, agricultural, and securities analysis is required before implementation.

Heirloom Asset Recovery: AI-assisted rediscovery of heritage apple varieties, old pears, grapes, berries, nut trees, rootstocks, and seed varieties surviving only in scattered private holdings, using historical newspapers, extension documents, nursery catalogs, plat maps, and aerial imagery, followed by respectful outreach, expert identification, and graft or scion preservation. Preservation credibility comes before aggressive commercialization.

6.4 What is NOT done

Status: concept defined, NOT productized. Legal and financial structure, including lending, licensing, referral, and securities questions, is unresolved. Partner economics, contracts, onboarding, and software are not designed. The liquidity graph data ontology is not built. The highest-tier items in the source handoff, the Liquidity-on-Demand platform, the signal network, the liquidity graph, and asset transition liquidity, are all conceptually defined with no product built. No partner has signed, no pilot has run, no transaction has closed under this program. Nothing in this section is a live offer.
