One page a buyer can act on and re-verify: the parcel, the ownership chain, the yield stack, the mineral overlay, the distress flags, and a hash-chained provenance receipt. Built from the pieces that already exist. Nothing here is live yet.

What the buyer gets

Six blocks, one page. Every block is either verified against a named source or labeled with exactly how it is not verified. The report is the product. The storefront sells the report.

BLOCK 01

Target Parcel

Parcel ID, acres (deeded), land use, section / township / range, and location. County parcel geometry and situs from the county record.

BLOCK 02

Ownership Chain

Holding entity to natural person to parcels. Resolved through the LandGraph-style pivot: LLC, registered agent, officer, person, full portfolio.

BLOCK 03

Yield Stack (Est.)

Modeled gross annual yield from stacked, non-conflicting uses, with the conflict rules that were enforced and the limiting notes that cut against it.

BLOCK 04

Mineral Overlay

Proximity score against heavy-mineral occurrences. A halo signal is a sampling priority, not a grade. No signal is reported as no signal.

BLOCK 05

Distress Flags

Tax delinquency, foreclosure activity, auction record, and owner-entity risk. Absence of a flag is stated as absence, not as proof of clean title.

BLOCK 06

Provenance Receipt

SHA-256 report hash over canonical content, PASS/FLAG verdict, timestamp, and the previous receipt hash. The buyer re-hashes to confirm.
