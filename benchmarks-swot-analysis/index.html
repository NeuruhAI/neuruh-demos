07How SWOT and benchmarking relate in this estate

SWOT and benchmarking are different tools, and this section keeps them apart. A SWOT is a 2-by-2 frame: internal strengths and weaknesses against external opportunities and threats. Benchmarking is comparative measurement: the same task, the same ground truth, scored the same way, repeated over time. The Bench does the second. The SWOT doc, whatever it contains, does the first, or claims to. Neither substitutes for the other, and a SWOT quadrant is not evidence that a benchmark score is good or bad.

The frame below is the standard SWOT frame with no content filled in. Each cell states its sourcing status instead of a finding. This is deliberate: the frame shows the reader exactly what a pulled SWOT would need to supply, and it prevents an empty-looking page from being mistaken for a page with nothing to say.
