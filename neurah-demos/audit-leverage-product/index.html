Figure 4

The wedge, property in to decision out

Do not turn this into "Neuruh Universal Autonomous Decision Intelligence Platform". That puts you right back into the trap. The outside market needs one pain, one workflow, one buyer, one proof packet, and one price. The commercial wedge is brutally simple: PROPERTY IN to DECISION OUT.

Send us an address or opportunity you are considering. We investigate the property, records, ownership, market, risks, economics and unresolved questions, then return a sourced acquisition decision package showing what we know, what we don't know, what can kill the deal, and what to do next.

That is understandable to a normal investor in fifteen seconds. You do not have to explain Neuruh, agents, AXON, Recipe Engine, or AI. The customer buys certainty before capital gets committed.

Externally, simply: DeedSonar Acquisition Dossier. Initially. No new consumer brand yet. The same primitive later becomes dossiers for FindSellProfit, ProofOS, Venture Factory, Transition Intelligence, CRE, Procurement, and Defense.

Figure 4. The wedge, turn 1 sections 5 and 6. Lead Recipe OS is the factory; the Decision Dossier is the first product off the factory line. Customers do not wake up wanting a recipe runtime; they wake up wondering "Should I buy this?"

Raw leads are cheap. Decision-ready opportunities are valuable. Executable opportunities are substantially more valuable. Closed outcomes create the moat.

Signal object and states

A signal must never automatically become a marketable opportunity. States: RECEIVED, TRIAGE, NEEDS_EVIDENCE, VERIFIED, REJECTED, SUPPRESSED, PROMOTED_TO_OPPORTUNITY. Signal quality is scored on inspectable components, never a single opaque AI score: identity confidence, evidence strength, novelty, economic potential, urgency, owner intent confidence, accessibility, transactionability, legal complexity, capital intensity, expected time to liquidity, market depth. Evidence classes stay FACT, DERIVED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN. Never convert speculation into fact.

One signal, many opportunities

A verified signal may produce 0, 1, or multiple opportunities. The filing's example: an older farmer wants cash but does not want to lose the farm. Do not reduce that to SELL FARM. Possible routes include selling unused equipment, leasing acreage, leasing storage, timber evaluation, partial parcel disposition, lot split, agricultural tenant, operating partnership, sale-leaseback, seller financing, conservation route, renewable-energy / infrastructure lease where appropriate, specialty crop opportunity, orchard rehabilitation, business succession, full sale, hold, or do nothing. The system's job is to find the best economic route subject to the owner's actual objective and constraints.

Figure 14

Capital, demand, and market mechanics

Liquidity on demand, separated from legal classification

A core long-term capability: an opportunity with enough verified economic value that qualified capital may be willing to provide liquidity before the final disposition. This is powerful but regulated. Separate the economic engine from the legal product classification. Do not silently implement cash advances. Do not originate a loan. Do not move production money. Do not create securities. Do not promise funding. Instead implement a neutral LiquidityOffer abstraction with economic structure, proposed amount, consideration, term, conditions precedent, required documents, legal classification, jurisdiction, counsel review required, and license requirement.

Founder capital thesis Conversation's own contemplated amounts

The founder contemplated being willing, in appropriate deals, to deploy amounts such as $10, $20, $50, or materially more, and in some opportunities potentially a portion of expected value or roughly half of a conservative economic amount. The software must NOT hardcode those numbers into underwriting. Instead build a CapitalMandate: provider, asset classes, jurisdictions, min and max check, max LTV or equivalent, max loss, required margin, max duration, allowed and prohibited structures, evidence floor, approval requirement. Then route opportunities against mandates.

Demand graph and market mechanics

Do not wait for opportunities before finding buyers. Build or consume a demand graph from buyer buy boxes: asset classes, geography, min and max value, condition, strategy, required margin, required yield, max complexity, title tolerance, response SLA, funding capacity, preferred routes. Track behavior, not claims: opportunities viewed, requested, offers, offer amount, conditions, response time, acceptance, close rate, funding speed, cancellations, disputes, actual purchases, actual outcomes where shared. The market is QUALIFIED OPPORTUNITY to MATCH ENGINE to RANKED COUNTERPARTIES to CONTROLLED DISTRIBUTION to OFFERS to ROUTE COMPARISON to OWNER / AUTHORIZED ACTOR DECISION. Ranking factors: price, net proceeds, certainty, speed, conditions, funding reliability, historical close probability, operational fit, distance, reputation, risk. No paid placement may secretly alter "best offer." V1 capital routing is PRIVATE, CURATED, PERMISSIONED, OPERATOR-CONTROLLED. Do not create a public investment marketplace in V1.

Figure 15

Owner protection, compliance, and privacy

The system must not become a machine for exploiting distressed owners. Every opportunity requires the owner's objective to remain explicit: needs cash fast, maximize price, keep property, keep farm, reduce workload, sell only unused asset, avoid debt, retire, transfer to family, remove liability, stop carrying cost, preserve legacy, or unknown.

The filing's example. Selling a $600,000 farm to solve a $20,000 liquidity problem may be economically irrational. The engine should identify lower-destructiveness routes first when they genuinely exist. Figures are the filing's example, not a case or an offer.

Compliance gate outcomes

Every transaction structure produces one of: ALLOW, ALLOW_WITH_CONDITIONS, HUMAN_REVIEW, COUNSEL_REVIEW, LICENSE_REQUIRED, BLOCK, UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN must never silently become ALLOW. Issues include real-estate brokerage, wholesaling, referral compensation, consumer lending, commercial lending, factoring, securities, investment solicitation, money transmission, escrow, title, insurance, privacy, telemarketing, DNC, consumer protection, elder exploitation, contract assignment, licensing, tax, agricultural regulations, and environmental restrictions.

Privacy

Do not build rumor trading, medical-condition targeting, protected-class targeting, stalking workflows, private-life surveillance, emergency-scene exploitation, or unauthorized personally identifying data harvesting. Signals must be purpose-bound and rights-aware. First responders or other public-safety professionals must not be incentivized to misuse confidential or non-public information acquired through official duties. If a partner category creates elevated conflict, ethics, or statutory risk: BLOCK OR REQUIRE FORMAL REVIEW.

Stack order, the strategic correction

The reply closes by correcting its own prior audit: Liquidity Engine + Signal Market is the new priority build. FSP is the first real asset-liquidity organ and the current closest software proof.
