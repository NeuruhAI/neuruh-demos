What this is

The estimator is a module named estimator inside landos-engine (FastAPI, port 8400), backed by Supabase, not a standalone service. Its pipeline, per the dossier: records plus photos, condition model, quantity takeoff, tier by store priced punch list, comp-anchored budget, priority plan, redesign spec, and SHA-256 provenance receipt.

It replaces prompt-guessed numbers with deterministic math over a versioned price book. Two lenses: buy-side max-offer validation (ARV minus rehab minus spread) and sell-side prep-to-list work ranked by ARV lift per dollar.

Counts verified by mechanical parse of the seed file in this demo build: 80 taxonomy codes (50 residential COMPONENT, 18 commercial ASSEMBLY, 12 industrial ALLOWANCE), 78 price book rows (26 components times 3 tiers, store BASE), 3 store multipliers, 16 labor rates, 24 unit cost models (8 asset classes times 3 scope bands). Total 201 value rows. Dossier-quoted, file-verified.

Gap the dossier records: 54 of the 80 taxonomy codes have no price book row yet. The Phase 3 research pass that would price them is not evidenced as run. Only the 26 priced components appear in the calculator below.
