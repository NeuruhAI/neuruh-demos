02Subject parcel

Address and parcel must be verified against at least 2 public sources before any number is used. Unverified means HOLD. Total acres must match the county record, survey, or FSA farm record.

03Acreage breakdown

Productive acres are tillable plus timber plus pasture. Wetland and waste acres are not productive. The breakdown must sum to total acres, and productive acres must be verifiable from a survey, county GIS, or FSA farm record. Unverifiable productive acres are a kill condition.

04Soil and productivity

Soil drives tillable value and cash rent. Web Soil Survey is the primary public source. A seller soil claim without a soil survey is a claim, not a fact.

05Cash rent

Cash rent is stated per tillable acre per year. Grade A is a signed lease in hand. B is a listed rent with a verifiable listing. C is a county rent survey or named source. D is a seller claim only, labelled rent proxy, never used as proven income.

Gross cash rent income = Tillable acres x Cash rent per acre

06Timber cruise value

Timber value comes from a cruise, not from a guess. A cruise needs a volume, a price basis, a date, and a named cruiser or source. A deal priced on timber value with no cruise is a kill.

Timber cruise value = Cruise volume x Price per unit (MBF or tons)

07Hunting and recreation value

Recreation value is lease income capitalized at a stated cap rate, or a stated per-acre value with a source. A lease rate from a single platform estimate is not proven income.

Recreation annual income = Recreation acres x Lease per acre
Recreation capitalized value = Recreation annual income / Cap rate

08Access, easements, and encumbrances

No legal access is a kill condition. Easement-only access, shared driveways, and recorded easements must be listed with their burden. If an easement is unknown, say so; do not assume the land is clear.

09Water and mineral rights

Rights are the E11 kill trigger. If the deal is priced on mineral or water value and the rights status is unknown, the run is a PASS. Severed rights are a fact to record, not a reason to guess the value.

10Land comps, vacant land and farm sales only

Minimum 3 usable land comps. Structure comps do not apply. A row is counted usable only when address, sale price, sale date, productive acres, and source URL are all filled. Price per productive acre is computed for each comp.

11Component valuation

Component value is the sum of the parts. Tillable land value uses a per-acre value from the land comps. Non-tillable base value covers timber and pasture acres at their land-only rate. Timber cruise value and recreation capitalized value are added on top. No component is counted twice. Every figure below is a MODELED ESTIMATE, NOT A VALUATION, NOT AN APPRAISAL.

Component value = (Tillable acres x Tillable $ / acre) + (Non-tillable productive acres x Non-tillable $ / acre) + Timber cruise value + Recreation capitalized value
Price per productive acre = Asking price / Productive acres
Price per total acre = Asking price / Total acres

12Highest and best use (HBU), four tests in order

The four tests run in order. A use that fails an earlier test is not tested further. Stated HBU must survive all four tests. If zoning or entitlement changes the HBU, run E1 Land and Zoning Unlock alongside this engine.

13Gates, fail closed

Any failed gate means HOLD. Component value is then shown only as a held number, never as a usable offer number. Authority is always no offer without Jeramie unless a human approval record is attached.

14Kill-switch

Check any condition that applies. A kill means the recommendation is PASS. A kill is a successful run, not a failed one. Three kills are also derived automatically from sections 08 and 09 and cannot be unchecked away: no legal access, and unknown mineral or water rights on a deal priced on them.

14How it works / What this is not / Sources


Read this before relying on any number on this page.


How it works: Enter verified facts with sources: the acreage breakdown, soil, cash rent, timber cruise, recreation lease, access and easements, and water and mineral rights. Enter at least 3 usable land comps. The tool computes productive acres, gross cash rent, timber cruise value, recreation capitalized value, component value, and price per productive acre from the exact formulas. Gates fail closed. A kill means PASS. The receipt records what was entered, what passed, and what was N/A. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers.


What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not a timber appraisal, soil report, or title opinion. Not legal, tax, or lending advice. Not a guarantee of value, rent, timber volume, or a sale. Not an offer. A component value above asking price does not mean a deal is good, and a PASS does not mean a property is bad. It means the evidence in this run does not support proceeding on the stated exit.

14Honest gaps and limits


What this tool does not do, stated plainly.


    

  • It does not verify a survey, a timber cruise, a soil report, or a title search. It trusts the numbers you enter and checks their arithmetic and their sources.
    • 

  • Wetland and floodplain acres are excluded from productive value but are not priced for mitigation cost.
    • 

  • Mineral and water rights are recorded as included, severed, partial, or unknown; the engine does not value them.
    • 

  • Split and entitlement upside is outside this engine; run E1 Land and Zoning Unlock for that.
    • 

  • Component value is N/A unless all four parts (tillable land value, non-tillable land value, timber cruise value, recreation capitalized value) are entered. A farm with no timber or no recreation must enter 0 for the missing parts to get a component value. That is the 2026-11-04 contract carried verbatim: a part with no evidence is N/A, not zero.
    • 

  • Grade D cash rent still computes gross rent income and rent yield; only the note labels it a rent proxy. The 2026-11-04 build computes it the same way, so this build carries it verbatim rather than inventing a rule that hides the number.
    • 

  • HBU order is not enforced: a later test can show Pass while an earlier test shows Fail, and the page reports "fails at least one of the four tests" rather than stopping at the first failure. The 2026-11-04 build behaves the same way; carried verbatim.
    • 

  • Sample C fires three kills, not two: the two checked boxes (productive acres unverifiable, mineral rights unknown) plus the auto-derived water-rights kill, because the deal is priced on mineral or water value and water rights are unknown. The 2026-11-04 sample note names only the two checked kills; the third kill is the auto-derived rule firing as written.
    • 

  • The acreage chart label for the longest bar can clip a few pixels past the right edge of the chart (Sample C tillable label "100.00 ac"). Cosmetic only; the numbers below the chart are exact.
    • 

  • No E11 specification document was found in the vault. Greps for E11 across the vault returned only the UnderwriteX.md line cited above, the 2026-11-04 E11 build itself, and unrelated hits (UUID fragments in drive-lane research files, Notion migration manifest IDs). The Codex V2 Appendix B cited as lineage in the 2026-11-04 build was not located in the vault. What would ground E11 further: the Codex V2 Appendix B document, or a written E11 spec from Jeramie stating the subject and mechanics.
    • 

  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Math was tested in Node with hand-computed values plus a jsdom DOM smoke test. See the build report.
    • 

  • All sample deals are synthetic and labeled as such.
