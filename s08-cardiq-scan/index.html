What the source builds did

Two Manus builds from June 2025: the first at cyckwhon.manus.space (React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, Shadcn, Vite) with a simulated camera, a simulated viewer count of 1,247, and mock prices; eBay, Vision, and Firebase integration points were named but not implemented, and the market-ready claim was the builder's claim. The updated build at ueevyeda.manus.space added a Stop Stream fix. Neither URL was verified live in this slice. This demo keeps the scan-flow idea and drops the pretense: everything here is labeled mock.

Disclaimer: this is a static simulation for demonstration only. It does not grade cards, does not read a camera, and its values must not be used to buy, sell, or price any card.
