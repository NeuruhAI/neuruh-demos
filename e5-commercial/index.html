05Tenant exposure and rent schedule

Exposure is the top tenant's share of gross potential rent. A single tenant over 50 percent is a concentration flag on its own, and a kill when combined with under 2 years remaining and no renewal evidence. The 5-year schedule grows gross potential rent at the rent-weighted escalation rate only. It assumes no rollover, no vacancy change, and no new leasing. It is a schedule, not a forecast.

13How it works / What this is not / Sources

Read this before relying on any number on this page.

How it works: confirm the property from at least 2 public sources, abstract every lease into the table in section 04, let the tool sum gross potential rent, apply vacancy, subtract the five expense lines to get NOI, then stabilize vacancy and expenses, divide stabilized NOI by the sourced cap rate for investment value, and price owner-user value separately from comparable sales per SF. Lease-up cost prices the vacant space. MAO applies buy percent to the chosen value basis and subtracts lease-up cost and the assignment buffer. Every number carries its source or is marked N/A. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not legal, tax, or lending advice, and not a replacement for reading the actual leases, a title commitment, an inspection, or a licensed professional. It does not invent tenants, rents, lease terms, cap rates, or prices. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. A high MAO does not mean a deal is good, and a PASS does not mean a property is bad. It means the evidence in this run does not support proceeding on the stated exit. No offer is quoted without Jeramie.

14Honest gaps and limits

What this tool does not do, stated plainly.

  • It does not underwrite debt: there is no DSCR, loan-to-value, or amortization math here, so a deal that passes this engine still needs lender underwriting. Use the E2 or E3 engine for debt math on the residential side.
  • It does not read leases for you: co-tenancy clauses, exclusivity, percentage rent, CAM caps, assignment language, and guarantor strength are not modeled, and any of them can change the value.
  • It does not verify tenant credit: a signed lease from a weak tenant is not the same as a signed lease from a strong one.
  • It caps the lease table at 5 tenant rows. More tenants need a combined row with a note, or a second run.
  • It uses one cap rate for the whole property, not a blended rate by tenant credit.
  • It does not model rollover risk beyond WALT and top-tenant exposure. The 5-year schedule assumes no rollover at all.
  • It does not model taxes, depreciation, or after-tax return. NOI here is pre-tax.
  • It does not check title, zoning, environmental condition, or floodplain status. Those need the E8 Distressed Title engine and a licensed professional.
  • No E5 specification document was found in the vault. Greps for E5 and Commercial across the vault returned only the UnderwriteX.md line cited in section 13, the 2026-11-04 E5 build itself, and unrelated hits (UUID fragments, drive-lane research files). The Codex V2 Appendix B cited as lineage in the 2026-11-04 build was not located in the vault. What would ground E5 further: the Codex V2 Appendix B document, or a written E5 spec from Jeramie stating the subject and mechanics.
  • An empty run (no lease rows entered) fires the lease-terms-unverified kill, so a blank page reads PASS, not HOLD. This is the 2026-11-04 contract carried verbatim: with zero rent rows there is no verified lease row. Enter at least one verified lease row to leave that state.
  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Math was tested in Node with hand-computed values, plus a jsdom DOM smoke test. See the build report.
  • All sample deals are synthetic and labeled as such.

E13E13 Risk / Kill-Switch

Every engine ends here. The first two kills are computed from the lease table and cannot be unchecked away: if the math fires them, they fire. The remaining kills are operator-checked. A kill is a successful run, not a failed one. Recommendation on a kill is PASS. Do not soften it.

Kill reasonsNone

RecommendationHOLD
Kill conditions (E5 contract, carried from the 2026-11-04 E5 build):
  • Single tenant over 50% of income with under 2 years term and no renewal evidence (computed from the lease table).
  • Lease terms unverified: any rent row marked Unverified, or no verified lease row (computed from the lease table).
  • Unconfirmed address / parcel (operator-checked).
  • Title defect with no cure cost (operator-checked).
  • Environmental flag with no professional report (operator-checked).
  • Any gate failure that leaves stabilized value or MAO as N/A on the stated exit (computed from the gates and the MAO).
Risk flags (not kills by themselves): top tenant over 50% of income with term or renewal keeping it from a kill, WALT under 3 years, occupancy under 85%, actual vacancy over 10%, cap rate source missing, stabilized value below owner-user value, second tenant over 30% of income, at least one renewal option unknown.
