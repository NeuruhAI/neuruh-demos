Neuruh Command Center

Your sovereign Bloomberg terminal. The spec's Phase 1 architecture: Supabase plus a custom Next.js terminal, a unified entities table, global search, dashboard panels, an entity explorer, an agent console, and a graph view. This page is the static demo of that terminal, seeded with the spec's own seed data.

Status: SIMULATION of the spec's Phase 1 terminal. No Supabase project is connected and no data is live. The entities below are the spec's own seed rows, copied from its SQL. Every revenue and count figure is a source claim from the January 2026 spec, not a verified fact: revenue $10,000 year to date, 5 active projects, 2 clients, 4 frameworks, and the 865-asset master inventory count. The spec's own price note was $25 per month for Supabase Pro, later revised in the same document to the free tier for the 48-hour MVP.
