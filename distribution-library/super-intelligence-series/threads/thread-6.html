Book 6 Thread: The Sovereign Assistant (@NeuruhAI)

Posts 1-10. Char counts verified below each post (all under 280).

1. The one app that runs your whole digital life is never shipping. In 2025 I designed it on paper: 200+ sections. In late 2026 the parts finally exist. The bus already left. It was a bus route. (191)

2. Three reasons the single app died, and they are structural: the integration economics never worked, the trust problem is unsolved at product scale, and the failure modes are personal. You cannot fix those with a bigger model. (225)

3. MCP changed the economics. Connecting a new data source went from "a project" to "a configuration." The maintenance moved to the server authors. Audit date: September 30, 2026. (176)

4. So stop waiting for the app. Build an organization instead: one supervisor that plans, delegates, and recovers from errors. Four specialists that each do one job well. This is Book 6 of The Neuruh Papers. (204)

5. The Archivist owns your memory. It walks a graph of people, documents, and commitments. Vector search finds text that resembles your query. The graph finds the answer. (167)

6. The Executive owns your commitments: email, calendar, scheduling. Its highest-value routine is the morning briefing, and a good briefing answers five questions and nothing else. Question five: what can be ignored, and why. (222)

7. The Analyst owns code execution. One rule: it never runs code on your bare machine. Ever. Language models do not do arithmetic. The model writes the code. The sandbox runs it. (175)

8. The Navigator clicks what cannot be fetched. Least reliable specialist by design, so it sits under a supervisor with a retry budget: three attempts, then it escalates to you. Build it last. (189)

9. Security is the feature, not a chapter. Four non-negotiables: device-flow OAuth, least privilege, human gates on every destructive action, sandboxed execution. The gate is a node in the graph, not a line in a prompt. (216)

10. Project Mnemosyne is a blueprint assembled from shipping components, not a single system anyone has shipped as a unit. A system has a plan for being wrong. A demo does not. Build the former. (190)
