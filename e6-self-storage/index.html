06Stabilized NOI and value

Deterministic math. Stabilized gross rent is PGI times stabilized occupancy. Stabilized EGI adds other income. NOI is EGI minus operating expenses. Value is NOI divided by the cap rate. No line is invented: a missing expense is N/A, and a missing cap rate leaves value N/A.

Stabilized GPR = PGI annual x Stabilized occupancy
Stabilized EGI = Stabilized GPR + Other income
Stabilized NOI = Stabilized EGI - Total operating expenses
Value = Stabilized NOI / Cap rate
Break-even occupancy = (Total operating expenses - Other income) / PGI annual

13How it works / What this is not / Sources

Read this before relying on any number on this page.

How it works: confirm the facility and parcel from at least 2 public sources, enter the unit mix from the rent roll, verify occupancy from the rent roll, collect competitor street rates from at least 2 distinct sources, then let the tool compute PGI, stabilized EGI, stabilized NOI, value at the stated cap rate, and break-even occupancy from the exact formulas in section 06. Expansion and remote management are priced separately and never folded into stabilized NOI. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a survey, not legal, tax, or lending advice, and not a replacement for county verification, a title commitment, a rent roll audit, or a licensed professional. It does not invent occupancy, rates, comps, or prices. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. A PASS does not mean the facility is bad. It means the evidence in this run does not support proceeding. No offer is quoted without Jeramie.

14Honest gaps and limits

What this tool does not do, stated plainly.

  • Break-even occupancy treats operating expenses as fixed at the entered level, so it understates break-even if expenses rise with occupancy.
  • Stabilized NOI uses street rates for occupied units, not collected rent, so delinquency and concessions are not in the rent line; delinquency is recorded in section 04 as a flag, not deducted.
  • Expansion yield uses a single expense percent and no lease-up timeline.
  • Remote readiness is a systems count, not a guarantee that remote management will work at this site.
  • Cap rate is an input, not a market finding; a wrong cap rate moves value directly.
  • It does not underwrite debt: there is no DSCR, loan-to-value, or amortization math here, so a deal that passes this engine still needs lender underwriting.
  • It does not model taxes, depreciation, or after-tax return. NOI here is pre-tax.
  • It does not check title, zoning, environmental condition, or floodplain status beyond the operator-checked title kill. Those need a licensed professional.
  • No E6 specification document was found in the vault. Greps for E6 and Self-Storage across the vault returned only the UnderwriteX.md line cited in section 13, the 2026-11-04 E6 build itself, and unrelated hits (an Anchorpoint board sprint label "E6 Docuseal peer sprint" and a 422 error-shape code "E6-shape", neither about self-storage). The Codex V2 Appendix B cited as lineage in the 2026-11-04 build was not located in the vault. What would ground E6 further: the Codex V2 Appendix B document, or a written E6 spec from Jeramie stating the subject and mechanics.
  • A blank run reads HOLD, not PASS: no kill fires until occupancy is marked unverified or rate comps rest on a single source. That is the 2026-11-04 contract carried verbatim.
  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Math was tested in Node with hand-computed values, plus a jsdom DOM smoke test. See the build report.
  • All sample deals are synthetic and labeled as such.

E13E13 Risk / Kill-Switch

Every engine ends here. The first two kills are computed from the occupancy verification and the rate sources and cannot be unchecked away: if the math fires them, they fire. The remaining kill is operator-checked. A kill is a successful run, not a failed one. Recommendation on a kill is PASS. Do not soften it.

Kill conditions (E6 contract, carried from the 2026-11-04 E6 build):
  • Occupancy unverifiable (computed from the occupancy verification in section 04).
  • Street rates from a single source (computed from the usable rate comps in section 05).
  • Title defect with no cure cost (operator-checked).
Risk flags (not kills by themselves): occupancy from owner statement only, occupancy from site count only, occupancy falling over 12 months, delinquency above 5 percent, fewer than 3 usable rate comps, expansion not permitted or unknown, operating expense ratio above 50 percent, stabilized NOI negative, remote readiness below 50 percent, unit mix row incomplete or occupied above count.
