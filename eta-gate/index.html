Why the denominator matters

If the formula parsed as (Iq·Ir·Iv)/Ewasted + Cviolations, adding violations would raise η - backwards. The pinned form divides by (1 + Ewasted + Cviolations), so violations only ever drag η down. One paren decides whether the objective rewards or penalizes constraint-breaking. Try it: raise Cviolations to 5 and watch η collapse.

The qubit

A knowledge qubit is a vector-quantized unit, not a scalar. Flag 2 in the session conceded the point: a "numerical value of text" is a shape with a modifier, living in a shared group. Everything inside one cell is treated as the same shape; intra-group distinctions are the loss. Who draws the cell boundaries? η.

Codebook: stub, not learned STUB-MODE

No real VQ codebook exists. The stub (_scripts/codebook.py) is deterministic hash-bucket centroids: SHA-256 of the group label → bytes → floats in [-1, 1], dim 16, mode stub-hashbucket. Same group in → same centroid out, every time. That makes qubits valid, provenance-verifiable, and inverse-able today.

The book example

Style compresses hard (cadence, grammar, an author's signature: high redundancy, low entropy, small prior). Content does not (the specific propositions are the incompressible surprise; Shannon's floor says only the unexpected part carries information). You cannot rebuild the book from style alone. You store style-prior (tiny, lossy) + content residual (what the prior gets wrong). That split is exactly why the system "stores and seeds" instead of just storing.
