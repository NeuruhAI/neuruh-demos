The gaps, stated plainly

Gap 1: 9 LandOS signals are unnamed in the sources read

The +40 signals in the LandOS codex dossier (§5) land on a 49-signal registry split 15 structure / 14 land / 14 owner-event / 6 market. That arithmetic leaves 9 original signals (1 structure, 2 land, 6 market) whose names were not recovered in the sources read for this build. They are listed here as UNKNOWN, not invented. Inventing 9 names to reach 49 would fabricate the inventory, which is the exact failure Q-SWEEP-4 warns about.

Gap 2: 49 vs 50 signals is unresolved

The registry says 49 active clean signals. The estate record says 50. The discrepancy recurs across batches 003, 004, 007, 008, 009 and 013. Do not resolve it by inventing a 50th signal; it needs one authoritative count. Until then, every count on this page is labeled with which source it came from.

Gap 3: the extractor arithmetic does not reconcile

The source states 37 of 49 active signals have a real extractor. Its own breakdown is 16 CV detectors plus 14 record signals plus 12 GIS / market signals, which sums to 42, not 37. This page reports both numbers and does not reconcile them. The 16 CV detectors are fetch plus harness only: each returns MISSING until trained weights are dropped into LANDOS_VISION_MODEL. Zero detections were invented.
