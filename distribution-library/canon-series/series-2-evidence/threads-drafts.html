Series 2 — Evidence Precedes Confidence: Threads drafts (@michianapicker)

Post 1: On June 30 I ran diligence on myself. $6M valuations, 801 IP entries, 858 assets, 130 repos I called products. None survived contact with running code. All voided. What survived: 43 working codebases, 4 live, 148 tests green, 49 signals, 43,897 records scored, 31 IP frameworks. And $0 MRR, counted in the same tier as the code. Evidence precedes confidence.

Post 2: The tier system that came out of it. Tier A: code-verified, claimable anywhere. Tier B: built and demonstrable, describable but not proof. Tier C: a document exists. Documents don't execute, never surfaced. Most founders pitch Tier C as Tier A. Check yours before someone else does.
