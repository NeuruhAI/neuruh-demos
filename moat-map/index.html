01The thesis

Deep art (especially through entertainment / memorable IP like characters, stories, etc), in-person experiences / events, commodities (the entire supply chain pretty much), land in future high-demand areas, any sort of high-quality services you can sell as high-ticket to people/companies who make money (most of them have way more money than time), anything with real scarcity, products/services sold through emotion, personalized goods, and one of my favorites: really good & well-polished software.

Imo there’s moat for everyone, but to Raul’s point it will take some agency & thinking + action to truly take advantage of those moats. Most people fall to fear, uncertainty and doubt, not to a lack of available opportunities.

Jeramie Hicks, November 20, 2026. Quoted in full, lightly punctuated for print.

The claim has two parts. First, moats are widely available, not rare. Second, the binding constraint is not opportunity, it is agency: thinking plus action, against fear, uncertainty, and doubt. This page tests the first part against Neuruh and the second part against Neuruh's own current bottleneck.

03The gate

The second half of the thesis is the diagnosis that matters most to Neuruh right now. The opportunities are inventoried: 595 segment items, 1,104 Master Map records, 336 live sites, three products with live checkout. What is missing is not opportunity. It is decisions.

As of November 20, 2026, 41 items sit on the founder's knock-out list, unanswered. Among them: the refund policy wording for the live store, the kernel fork for the estate sale, the chain and wallet pick that gates the on-chain anchor, the Stripe reconnect follow-through, the LLC filing, and the four paid buyers waiting on ship-or-refund answers. Each one is small. Together they are the gate. Fear, uncertainty, and doubt do not arrive as a single dramatic moment. They arrive as 41 small unanswered questions.

The thesis says a moat takes agency, thinking, and action. The map says Neuruh already holds four moats and two partial ones. The gate says the distance between holding a moat and taking advantage of it is measured in answered decisions, not in new opportunities.

04What closes the gaps

Each gap has a next action that already exists. None of them needs a new opportunity.

  • Land moat not producing: restore DeedSonar on free platforms. A new Vercel account and a new free Supabase project, migrations applied, three environment variables set, deploy, verify live. The lead estate starts empty because both old Supabase projects are dead. Say so plainly when it ships.
  • High-ticket services at $0: the offers are live and checkout works. What is missing is buyers, and the buyer path is the free 10-minute qualifier at cal.com/neuruh. The store takes real money now. The next sale is the proof.
  • Well-polished software, the weakest moat: polish is a sequence, not a wish. Unified Chain v2 is the template: real logic, real tests, browser verified, live. The next candidate is the store and the Scan flow, then DeedSonar itself once it is restored.
  • IP and emotion as products: the material exists. What is missing is a product shape: a character, a story, or a dossier that a buyer can pay for. The Ananias writing and the Arena note are the raw material, not the product.
  • Commodities beyond early: one grower and one sell sheet become a supply chain only with volume and repeat buyers. First Pick needs its second grower and its first repeat order.
  • In-person experiences: nothing built, nothing owed. This is the one category where the honest answer is to leave it empty until a real opportunity appears, not to invent one.
  • The gate itself: answer the knock-out list by number, or say go through them. The list is the work.
