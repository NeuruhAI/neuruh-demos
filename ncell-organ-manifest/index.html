N-Cell Organ Manifests

This page demonstrates what an organMask byte means once an Organ Manifest exists. It stamps headers for display only. It enforces nothing, routes nothing, and grants no authority.

Provenance. Organ roles and non-negotiable boundaries are quoted verbatim from Section 4 of the N-Cell position paper, https://ncell-position-paper.pages.dev/ (accessed 2026-11-14). The paper defines the N-Cell/64 header byte fields in Section 3.3 but does not define per-organ bit meanings. Every organMask bit assignment on this page is the manifest's own mapping, labeled as such. It is never a paper claim. Where the paper is silent, the manifest says unresolved instead of inventing a value.

3. Stamp an N-Cell/64 header

Header layout, paper Section 3.3: byte 0 routeMask, byte 1 organMask, byte 2 lifecycleMask, byte 3 authorityMask, byte 4 memoryMask, byte 5 confidence 0-255, byte 6 risk 0-255, byte 7 outputMask. Byte 1 is computed from the checked capability families above. The other bytes are yours to set; this demo does not validate them against the manifest, because the paper assigns no per-organ values for them.

Topology delta (paper Section 3.5)

Delta is the XOR of successive headers: delta = header XOR previous header. A changed bit names a changed byte, not a reason. The receipt must still retain semantic evidence: why a route changed, which policy acted, which model proposed the change, and what outcome followed.

Governance intersection (paper Section 3.4)

Executable topology is the intersection of proposed, allowed, and available capability: E* = E_proposed AND E_allowed AND E_available. This demo does not compute that intersection. Stamping a header here is not approval, and an organMask bit set here grants nothing.
