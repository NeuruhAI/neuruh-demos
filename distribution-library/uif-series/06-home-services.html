Home Services — Your AI Receptionist Just Quoted a Panel Upgrade

For: electrical, HVAC, and plumbing contractors, 3–40 trucks Alt headline: It Answered the Phone. Then It Made You a Promise. Keyword: AI receptionist for contractors

An AI receptionist is the easiest win in home services. It picks up at 9 p.m., books the call, and never puts anyone on hold.

Until a homeowner asks, "Roughly what does a panel upgrade run?" and the receptionist, trying to be helpful, says "$1,800, and we can do it Thursday." Your real number is double that, Thursday is full, and the job needs a permit and a utility disconnect anyway. Now your tech walks in already wrong. The homeowner has a recording.

The fields that broke: Price, date, and scope — three promises the receptionist was never authorized to make.

The missing pins: Randomness, tool access, and the fuse. The receptionist was allowed to improvise on money, it could talk about dates it couldn't actually book, and nothing checked a number before it was spoken.

The fix: - Prices come from your price book or not at all. No listed price means one fixed line: "The technician will quote that on site." - Fixed answers stay fixed. Pricing, warranty, and service-area answers are scripted word for word, not generated fresh each call. - It books only what's open. Calendar access is limited to real open slots; it can't promise a day it can't book. - Emergencies interrupt. Words like gas, sparking, or flooding route straight to your on-call line and your approved safety script. - Every call is logged with what was said, so a dispute is a lookup, not an argument.

The receptionist keeps booking jobs at 9 p.m. It just stops writing checks your techs have to cash.

Where else this shows up: Anywhere an AI speaks for you, the question is what it's authorized to promise. A listing tool naming a maker (Resale), an appeal letter citing a policy (Healthcare Admin), a receptionist quoting a price: each is a commitment made without authority. Write down what your system may commit to, and treat everything else as a handoff to a person.
