How to read this report

Each segment inventory lists every item its agent found, with a one-line summary, a live URL or file path, and an honest state from the ladder: CONCEPT, DESIGNED, SCAFFOLDED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, INTEGRATED, DEPLOYED, LIVE, OBSERVED, PROVEN, COMMERCIAL, BLOCKED, STALE, SUPERSEDED, DUPLICATE, ARCHIVED, UNKNOWN. Implemented is not deployed is not live is not proven. A browser simulation is labeled as such. A draft is a draft. Queued is not published.

Two method notes apply to every total in this report. First, the segment totals are line-item counts, not deduplicated counts. The same hub site is cross-listed in more than one segment inventory where it plausibly belongs to both, and the reconciliation below measures that overlap rather than hiding it. Second, where an agent could not verify a fact, the item is marked UNVERIFIED or UNKNOWN in its inventory with what would verify it. Those marks are carried into this report, not resolved by guessing.

Reconciliation: 595 line items vs 192 hub sites

The gap is real and it is explained, not a counting error. The 595 is a gross line-item count across 8 inventories. The 192 is the count of live sites on the hub. They measure different universes, and three effects bridge them.

1. Cross-listing. 231 distinct URLs appear across the 8 inventories. 87 of those URLs appear in more than one inventory. The largest overlaps are Tools x AI Systems (45 shared URLs), Tools x Research (16), Tools x Real Estate (13), Real Estate x AI Systems (9), and AI Systems x Research (5). Per-file unique URL counts sum to 326 occurrences against 231 distinct URLs, so about 95 occurrences are cross-listings of the same site in a second segment. This was by design in the briefs (an item plausibly belonging to two segments was listed in both rather than dropped), but it means the 595 overstates distinct things.

2. Non-hub items. 47 inventory URLs are not hub sites (YouTube, Spotify, cal.com, Stripe, Substack, and similar). Beyond URLs, roughly 269 line items have no URL at all: documents, manuscript files, repos, cron jobs, loops, offers, groups, drafts, and papers. The inventories cover far more than the hub's live sites, which is correct for a build inventory, and it is the second reason the total exceeds 192.

3. Per-unit line items. Some inventories count units the hub counts once or not at all: 16 shipped videos, 6 Neuruh Papers manuscripts, 106 queued X drafts (counted as one queue item in segment 07 but as content in segment 03), 5 SpaceXSI resumes, 30 convergence items finished in code, 118 Done Ledger items (counted as one ledger item here), and similar. The hub is a map of live sites. The inventories are a map of built things. They should not match.

Hub coverage check. Of the 192 hub sites, 184 appear by URL in at least one inventory. Three more appear by name without a URL (founder-story-bza, world-apg, world-dispatch-iq). One more is inventoried as an item under a different name (newstyle-gallery, inventoried as meme gallery v12 with its URL unlocated). Four hub sites are absent entirely, by URL and by name: invisible-physics-of-genius, liability-layer, neuruh-constitution, personal-executive-agent. Those four need a segment assignment or an explicit decision that they are out of scope.

Deduplicated total. A true deduplicated count of distinct built things was not produced in this pass, because it needs a name-based dedup across the non-URL items, which the segment agents did not do. The honest bounds are: at least 231 distinct URLs plus roughly 269 non-URL line items, minus cross-listed non-URL items (the same document, repo, or ledger listed in two segments without a URL). A reasonable estimate is roughly 500 distinct things, but that number is an estimate, not a verified total. The missing step is a name-based dedup pass over the 8 inventories. Until that pass runs, quote 595 as "595 line items across 8 inventories, with known cross-listing," never as "595 distinct things built."
