About this demo

Formulas are the spec’s own: TotalRehabCost = sum of component cost x risk factor x local unit cost. Roof $7.50 x roof sqft; HVAC $4,500 to $7,000 (uses $5,750 midpoint); electrical $3,000 to $8,000 (uses $5,500); plumbing $2,500 to $6,000 (uses $4,250); foundation $8,000+; interior $20 to $50 per sqft; cleanup and unknowns 10 to 15% buffer (uses 12%). Exit rules are the spec’s rule-based selector, quoted in the item doc. This page proves the logic runs; it does not pull live parcel, permit, FEMA, or market data. That connection is a founder decision, not a demo feature.
