01 The Play

The source positions the network as a launchpad, not a casual meetup. Its words: "We’re not another casual ‘let’s grab coffee’ meetup. This is a launchpad for founders, engineers, creators, and investors who want to build, scale, and own the next wave of Michiana’s tech economy."

"The future of Michiana will be built here, by us."

The same closing passage appears twice in the source, word for word, under two separate "Closer" headings: "We’re building this together, but the window to shape it is short. If you’re a builder, backer, or operator with ambition, you should be in the room from day one." That duplication is in the source document itself, not an error in this page.

02 What It Is Building

Three elements, as stated in the source:

  • A founder-first network. Connect the people who are actually doing or beginning the work.
  • Cross-pollination across industries: AI, advanced manufacturing, SaaS, e-commerce, defense tech, real estate tech, and whatever is next.
  • A deal flow pipeline. Founders meet investors, talent meets opportunity, projects meet capital.

03 Why This Matters

The source gives three reasons:

  • Michiana sits on untapped industrial, academic, and capital resources, but no one is connecting the dots.
  • Chicago, Indy, and Detroit are 90 minutes away, but there is no reason all the good ideas should leave here.
  • The next decade belongs to operators, not spectators.

No figures support these claims in the source. No resource inventory, no capital total, and no count of ideas leaving the region are given. They are stated as the draft’s argument, not as verified facts.

04 What Members Get Access To

  • Monthly Flagship Nights: one keynote, one panel, one pitch session. Every month. No filler.
  • Weekly: TBD. The source lists the word "Weekly" with no format, day, or topic attached. The weekly item is unspecified.
  • Rapid-Fire Deal Tables: pre-vetted founders meet funders and collaborators.
  • Build Weekends: 48-hour sprints to launch MVPs, solve real problems, and form new ventures.
  • Private Founder Circle: group chat plus invite-only dinners and more.

05 Who Should Be Here

The source names five groups:

  • Founders and co-founders, pre-seed to growth stage
  • Engineers, product designers, and technical builders
  • Angel investors, VCs, and corporate innovation leads
  • Operators who have scaled companies and want to mentor
  • Students and researchers looking to commercialize work

No individual in any group is named in the source. No member count is given.
