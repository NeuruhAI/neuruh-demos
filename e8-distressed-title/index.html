03Lien stack in priority order

Priority order is the order liens are paid at closing, senior first. Property taxes are normally senior to a mortgage. A lien with no amount or no source is not a complete lien, and an incomplete stack kills the deal. Every row needs a lien type, an amount, a source, and a verified flag. Rows are reordered by the Priority number you enter.

08Closing structure: assignment vs double close

The structure follows the title facts, not the fee. Assignment is only recommended when title is clearable, no probate is pending, cure cost is small, and the score is 70 or higher. Everything else that is still dealable goes double close, so the title company clears title on the first leg.

Assignment if ALL of: dealability score 70 or higher, no kill fired, probate status is none or open with PR appointed, cure cost $5,000 or less, net equity positive.
Double close if ANY of: score below 70, probate not opened or heir consent incomplete, quiet title needed, cure cost above $5,000, title company requires it, or assignment fee disclosure risk is flagged.
PASS if ANY kill fired. No structure is recommended on a PASS.

13How it works / What this is not / Sources

Read this before relying on any number on this page.

How it works: confirm the parcel from at least 2 public sources, list every lien in priority order with an amount and a source, grade probate and tax risk, price every cure line, then let the tool compute total liens, gross equity, cure cost, cure ratio, net equity, and the dealability score from the stated rubric. The closing structure follows the title facts: assignment only when title is clearable, no probate is pending, cure cost is $5,000 or less, and the score is 70 or higher. A share link encodes the whole deal in the URL so someone else can open the same numbers.

What this is not: not an appraisal, not a title commitment, not legal or tax advice, and not a replacement for a title search, a probate attorney, a tax collector payoff, or a licensed professional. It does not invent liens, prices, heirs, or payoff amounts. If a field cannot be verified, it stays N/A. A high dealability score does not mean a deal is good, and a PASS does not mean a property is bad. It means the title evidence in this run does not support proceeding. No offer is quoted without Jeramie.

14Honest gaps and limits

What this tool does not do, stated plainly.

  • It does not pull live county records, title searches, tax payoffs, or probate dockets; every fact is entered by hand and must be checked at its source.
  • Lien priority is a legal determination that varies by jurisdiction and recording order; this tool orders rows by the priority number you enter and does not verify priority itself.
  • IRS lien payoff amounts, redemption figures, and probate timelines change with the county and the case; enter the figures the county or the attorney gives you, or leave them N/A.
  • The dealability score is a screening rubric built for this engine, not a market standard.
  • No E8 specification document was found in the vault. Greps for E8 as a whole word and for "Distressed Title" across the vault returned zero hits outside unrelated files. The Codex V2 Appendix B cited as lineage in the 2026-11-04 build was not located in the vault; the only "Codex V2" mentions in the vault are a recipe count line and a recovery ledger title, neither an E8 spec. What would ground E8 further: the Codex V2 Appendix B document, or a written E8 spec from Jeramie stating the subject and mechanics.
  • FIXED in this build, defect 1 (2026-11-04 script): loading a sample did not rebuild the lien and cure rows. Sample C adds a fifth lien row (HOA, $3,500); loading Sample A or Sample B afterward kept that row, so total liens read $100,500 instead of $97,000 for Sample A and $82,000 instead of $78,500 for Sample B, corrupting equity and score. This build rebuilds the rows at the start of every sample load. The 2026-11-04 build and its local file were not touched.
  • FIXED in this build, defect 2 (2026-11-04 script): the combined kill list dedup compared short automatic labels ("Lien stack incomplete") against long manual labels ("Lien stack incomplete (E8 kill condition 1)") with indexOf, which never matches, so a kill fired both ways appeared twice in kill_conditions_fired. This build drops an automatic entry when a manual label starts with it. The manual and automatic lists are still shown separately on the page.
  • Carried quirk, not fixed: on a blank page the dealability score reads 100 / 100, because no deduction applies with no inputs. The recommendation is still HOLD until the gates and required fields are filled. This is the 2026-11-04 behavior carried verbatim.
  • Carried behavior: net equity stays N/A until a cure total and a repairs figure are both entered, even when the cure total would be zero. A blank cure table is not the same as a zero cure cost in this engine.
  • Layout was not eyeballed in a real browser. Math was tested in Node with hand-computed values plus a jsdom DOM smoke test. See the build report.
  • All sample deals are synthetic and labeled as such.
