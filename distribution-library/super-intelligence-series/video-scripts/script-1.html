Script 1: Stop Picking the Biggest AI Model (60-90 second talking head)

Stop picking the biggest AI model. Seriously. The leverage moved, and most people missed it.

Here is what happened. In 2025 I wrote that obsessing over AI parameter counts would be a relic by 2026, that architecture would replace anatomy. Last September I audited my own prediction. It held.

The top models all got good at the same things. So the difference between winning and losing moved to the architecture around the model, how you structure what the AI reads. And here is the physics nobody priced in: every time you change the beginning of a prompt, the AI re-reads everything after it. A timestamp header does that on every single turn. Same model, same answers, up to twenty times the cost. Just from word order.

The fix is boring and it works. Stable instructions first, never changed. History appended, never rewritten. Volatile stuff last. Three layers. That is the whole architecture.

The model is the engine now. Engines commoditize. Own the layer above it. That is where the margin lives.
