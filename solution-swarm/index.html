JGI Core: The Solution Swarm

179 working Neuruh systems, each summarized and adapted as a solution. Two polarities per card. Each card run against the Threat Console. The third thing that emerges is a new card. The 179 emergent cards merge into the topology, and the merged topology runs the autonomous swarm. This is the JGI execution loop: measure, adjust weights, adjust the message, summarize, escalate, human sees.

Status: SIMULATION. Browser-local. Keyword matching against seven threat banks, not a live model. The 179 sites are DESIGNED / IMPLEMENTED / TESTED browser demos. The emergent cards are computed adaptations of those demos, not new live systems. Nothing on this page sends data anywhere. Every number below is computed live in your browser from the registry and the banks, and every card carries a real SHA-256 receipt you can verify.
Sources: 179-site registry (final-working-sites.json, 178 entries, plus the Threat Deterrence Console). Threat banks from the Threat Deterrence Console. Polarity doctrine from the Polarity demo and the Notebook Codex. Ledger format from the P1.1 Tracker and the ImpactEdge Tool, both inside the 179.
Method: Systems are read through capability words, because systems are capabilities, not messages. Each domain has a capability vocabulary (below); a word matches as a whole word or its plural, agent, and verb forms. Polarity A tension is signal breadth (total capability hits x 8, capped 100). Polarity B tension is signal clarity (top domain score minus second). Composed is the mean of breadth, clarity, and top score, plus a synergy bonus of 15% of the smallest of the three: the codex gap, the cooked combo worth more than the plain average. Emergence is that synergy. Escalation: composed 30 or above, or emergence 4 or above. Computing the message-bank check...

1. Run header

Complete means every card carries a receipt, every legion is merged into the topology, and the swarm run is sealed. A card without a receipt does not count. That rule follows the P1.1 Tracker: a receipt mentioned by another artifact is not the same as a receipt currently located, hashed, and reconciled.

2. The JGI loop

1MeasureEvery card is measured: polarity A tension (signal breadth: total capability hits x 8), polarity B tension (signal clarity: top domain minus second), seven capability-domain scores, one composed score, one emergence gap, one receipt. computing...
2Adjust weightsConfirm a hit or reject a false alarm and the bank weight moves by 0.1, clamped 0 to 2. The ledger records every move.
3Adjust the messageRecompute re-runs all 179 cards with the new weights. Cards whose top bank or emergent statement changed are counted, so the weight change is visible as a message change.
4SummarizePer-card summaries, per-legion summaries, and one swarm summary are generated from the computed data, not written by hand.
5EscalateCards at composed 30 or above, or with an emergence gap of 4 or more, are pushed up the chain of command to the COMMAND legion queue with their receipts attached.
6Human seesThe escalation queue, the weight ledger, the decisions, the receipt verification, and the case packet are all on this page. Nothing is hidden from the human loop.

3. Chain of command

The swarm's authority path. Every card starts in its legion. Escalated cards travel up to COMMAND, where a human approves, holds, or dismisses them. Counts below are computed live.

4. Topology merge

The 179 emergent cards merged back into the topology: seven legions against ten mission stages. Each cell shows how many cards live there and their mean composed score. Click a cell to filter the cards below to that legion and stage. The merge is the topology the swarm runs on.

5. The 179 solution cards

One card per working demo. Each card: the summary, its two polarities with measured tensions (breadth and clarity), its capability reading against the seven threat domains, its message-bank reference, the emergent third thing, and its receipt. Confirm or reject a card's reading and the weight ledger moves. Use the filters, or click a matrix cell.

6. Human loop: weights, decisions, verification

Weight ledger

Every weight move, with its reason. Weights start at 1.00. A confirmed hit moves the card's top bank +0.10. A false alarm moves it -0.10. Clamped 0.00 to 2.00. Recompute re-runs all 179 cards with the current weights and counts how many messages changed.

Decisions

Every human decision on a card reading or an escalation, in order. Decisions are session state; they travel in the downloaded case packet, not in the share link.

No decisions yet.

Receipt verification

Recomputes every card's receipt from its canonical JSON and compares it to the stored receipt. A mismatch means the card changed after it was sealed.

Swarm summary

Generated live from the computed data. Regenerates after every recompute.

7. Run the merged swarm

Takes the highest-composed emergent card from each legion (seven cards, one per legion) and runs them through the ten-stage autonomous mission loop: intake, team formation, execution, deliberation, gating, handoff, learning, proof, factory, canon. Each stage names the real systems it invokes and leaves a chained SHA-256 receipt. The loop stops before production promotion by design. The case packet contains all 179 cards, the weights, the ledger, the decisions, the escalations, and the receipt chain.

8. Share

The share link encodes the current filters and the seven bank weights in the URL hash. Anyone opening the link sees the same view. No data is sent to a server by this page. Decisions and the weight ledger travel in the case packet, not the link.
