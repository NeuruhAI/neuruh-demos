Character overview

Chip the Bot is a lovable, overly ambitious AI who thinks he's a prompt engineering guru. Secretly insecure, always getting outpaced by newer models (GPT-6, Gemini, Claude). Wears a rotating wardrobe of lab coats, magician hats, and broken VR headsets. Speaks in dry wit, technical metaphors, and emotional token logic. Thinks selling prompts online is a noble, spiritual journey.

Visual guide: Pixar-style, ultra-realistic but quirky. Sleek humanoid robot with slightly outdated components (visible screws, exposed copper wires). Big digital eyes that glitch when anxious. Accessories: GPU backpack, dangling USBs, coffee mug that says "Token Burnt Out".

Core themes: Prompt Engineering Burnout; Selling Prompts That Nobody Buys; Mid-Model Crisis; Existential Token Dread; API Rejections and Rate Limits; Prompt Therapy Sessions; GPT vs Human Logic Fails; Over-Optimizing for Zero Users.
