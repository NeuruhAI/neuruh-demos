§1-2The Hypothesis and Its Nine-Stage Mechanism

LCDE v0.1 — When an adaptive agent develops deep representations across multiple structurally distinct domains before encountering a future target domain, recurring relational invariants may become encoded as latent schemas. When a novel target creates sufficient structural resonance and productive polarity, the schemas can recombine and reorganize into an integrated capability requiring less target-specific learning and producing broader transfer than equivalent target exposure without the prior structure.

Run the mechanism. Each stage is necessary in the hypothesis; remove one and the chain is the control condition, not the phenomenon.

Idle. Case interpretation (§3): Jeramie may have approached AI not as a blank-slate software learner but carrying deeply encoded schemas from physical markets, real estate, operations, defense, self-modeling, content, and IP packaging — with AI as the enabling medium in which dormant structures could be externalized and recombined. Plausible formulation. Not evidence.
