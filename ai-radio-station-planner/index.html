AI Radio Station Planner

From the Drive spec “24/7 ‘AI + AI&Me + Me-only’ radio/talk station” (2025-08-02). The spec ranks three architectures by speed-to-air and details the setup for each. This page turns that into a picker plus a one-hour clock builder: choose the architecture, lay out the segments, and export the clock. It streams nothing. It is a planner, not a station.

Planner Spec architectures

1. Pick an architecture (spec ranked by speed-to-air)

Option A. Free, fastest launch

Local PC to YouTube Live. OBS Studio + PlayIt Live or Mixxx + Piper TTS or XTTS-v2. Cost $0. Live as long as the PC stays on. YouTube enforces copyright and live policy strictly.

Option B. Free, real radio

Local PC to a free Icecast host (e.g. Caster.fm). BUTT or Mixxx encoder + PlayIt Live or Mixxx playout. Cost $0. Free plans may reset, inject ads, or limit direct stream links.

Option C. Near-free, pro control

$5 to $10 VPS + AzuraCast (Docker). Icecast/Shoutcast, Liquidsoap AutoDJ, scheduling, web player, SFTP, REST APIs, webhooks. Spec’s recommended minimum for a serious 24/7 channel.

2. Build one hour of clock

The station has three voices: AI-only, AI+Me, Me-only. Add segments until the hour totals 60 minutes. Segment names and lengths are yours; the defaults below follow a talk-station shape.

About this demo


Setup steps for the chosen architecture are the spec’s own, summarized in the detail box. Nothing here creates a stream key, installs AzuraCast, or goes live. Going live is a founder step: it needs a YouTube or Icecast account, a machine that stays on (Options A and B), and a copyright-clean playlist. Keep streams free of copyrighted music; the spec warns YouTube will scan and can interrupt or terminate the stream.
