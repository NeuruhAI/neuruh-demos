Fig. 3. The imagery tier stack

Imagery provenance is the one-shot decision: a poisoned training corpus cannot be cleaned retroactively. NAIP is flown for agriculture, so it covers the rural fringe — pole barns, farms, estates — better than any paid product. That is the highest-intent target class, and it runs on free, clean, national data.

TIER 0 — NAIPFREE · BACKBONE · ON

USDA aerial imagery, public domain, ML-training clean. Entire continental US, 2–3 year refresh, 30–60cm. Runs on every parcel. Drives the triage score. Outbuilding detection, driveway geometry, vehicle / equipment presence, pool, lot context, water adjacency, vegetation state.

TIER 1 — NearmapPAID · CONFIRM · GATED

4.4–7.5cm, 87% of US population, 130+ pre-built AI property attributes (roof type, material, condition, tree overhang) already trained. Called only on parcels that already score high on free Tier 0. Triage free, confirm paid. Switched off until funded.

STREET-LEVELDEFERRED

No clean, free, national street-level source for model training exists. Google Street View is ToS-blocked for ML derivatives. Mapillary and KartaView are CC-BY-SA — share-alike forces derivatives open. Live-only as a human decision aid via licensed API, never ingested into the proprietary dataset.

Amber tier — parked and unscored

Built as property-event captures but NOT in the active signal array, so the engine never calls them. Flipping any into scoring is one registry edit, gated behind a disparate-impact legal review. That switch is Jeramie's, not the build's.

Fig. 4. What the score is for

Score tells you which parcels. The graph (LandGraph, the entity-resolution layer built beside this engine) tells you who is behind them and what else they own. Record signals first — tax delinquency, probate stage, pre-foreclosure, USPS vacancy, tenure are the top weights, free, structured, and need no computer vision. NAIP-CV second, geospatial joins third, Nearmap last. The labeled dataset the feedback loop accrues — score, contact, close, realized margin — is the long-game asset: years out, that corpus is worth more than the SaaS. The Indiana layer is proven by a live Elkhart parcel — 20-06-05-388-014.000-012, 418 W Marion, class 520 — identity only: no owner, no sale, no value, no delinquency. Berrien MI has no reachable public ArcGIS REST.
