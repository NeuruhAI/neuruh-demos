Appliance ROI Calculator

Scores a kitchen appliance on the ROI formula from the source module: use frequency, time saved, batch prep, annual savings, and repair cost against lifespan. Built for the KitchenGPT Appliance_ROI_Analyzer logic layer.

Calculator

Source formula

ROI Score = (Monthly Use Frequency x Time Saved %) + (Batch Prep Score x Max Servings per Use) + (Estimated Annual Savings) - (Avg Repair/Replacement Cost / Lifespan)

Normalized to a scale of 1-10 in the source.

Normalization note: the source states scores are normalized to 1-10 but gives no normalization constants, so the source's table scores (9.4, 9.2, and so on) cannot be reproduced from the formula as written. This demo therefore reports the raw score and bands it on the raw scale (150+ excellent, 100-150 strong, 50-100 moderate, under 50 weak). Those bands are a demo interpretation, stated here, and the demo does not claim to reproduce the source's 1-10 scores.
