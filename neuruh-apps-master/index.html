1. How to read this index

An app is listed once. Its state uses the estate ladder, and the ladder is strict: DESIGNED is not DEPLOYED, DEPLOYED is not LIVE, LIVE is not PROVEN, and nothing here is COMMERCIAL.

  • CONCEPT: named idea, no design artifact on record.
  • DESIGNED: design or spec documents exist; no working build verified.
  • IMPLEMENTED: code or a working build exists; deployment or live use not verified.
  • TESTED: a build passed stated tests; not confirmed live.
  • DEPLOYED: placed on a host; live response not verified on the audit date.
  • LIVE: returned HTTP 200 to a curl check on 2026-11-18, or is a live service on record with a working URL.
  • PROVEN: live use with receipts over time. No app in this index meets it.
  • COMMERCIAL: taking money. No app in this index meets it. MRR $0.
  • BLOCKED: a named blocker stops it: deployment paused, access unresolved, or a dependency down.
  • UNKNOWN: the sources in hand do not establish the state. Contents unpulled, no live URL found, or the live check failed without a diagnosable reason. UNKNOWN is a verdict, not a gap to fill with a guess.

Two rules govern every entry. First, a Drive file title is evidence of a file, not of an app: the four anchor files are 1.0KB Google Docs, and their contents were not pulled, so no claim about what the apps do can be grounded beyond their titles. Second, a demo is labeled a demo: a browser page that simulates a product is recorded as a simulation, and the product keeps its own state.
