Series 2 — Evidence Precedes Confidence: Facebook post draft (personal profile)

Two years of building, and on June 30 I finally ran an adversarial diligence check on myself. I had numbers I used to say: $6M valuation, 801 IP entries, 858 assets, 130 repos I called products. None of it survived contact with running code. All voided. Kept for history, never cited again.

What survived is better. 43 working codebases. 4 live. 1 deployed engine. 148 tests green on the scoring model. 49 signals, 43,897 records scored. 31 named IP frameworks. And $0 MRR, which I count in the same tier as the code, because the honest zero is what makes the rest believable.

New rule for everything I build from here: no dollar valuation is claimable before revenue exists. Evidence precedes confidence. That's the series I'm writing. This is part 2.
