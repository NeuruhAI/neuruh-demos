DIVISION I

Why multiple metaphors appeared

Each metaphor exposes a different design problem.

The conversation moved through Worlds, galaxies, atoms, DNA, proteins, bacteria, brain, nervous system, blood, heart, arms, legs, and physical reality. That movement was not random. Each metaphor exposes a different design problem: scale, composition, specialization, signaling, resource flow, command, protection, adaptation, and emergence.

The danger is literalizing the metaphors into duplicate software: one "atomic" registry, another "DNA" engine, a "brain" router, a "bacteria" swarm, and a "world" store that all represent overlapping truth. The cure is one formal ontology with multiple human-readable views.

Invariant

Metaphor is not ontology. Every named thing must answer seven questions:

Q1

What is its canonical ID?

Q2

Who writes its state?

Q3

What contract does it expose?

Q4

What evidence supports it?

Q5

What authority does it have?

Q6

What resources does it consume?

Q7

What outcomes retire or strengthen it?

Microbiome admission rules: what an external provider must satisfy to extend capacity

  1. External model or provider has a typed contract and limited authority.
  2. Data rights, privacy, retention, cost, latency, coverage, and failure are known.
  3. Outputs are evidence, candidate transformations, or proposals, not unquestioned truth.
  4. Health and drift are monitored; fallback or removal is possible.
  5. The organism keeps canonical identity and state and can change providers without losing its memory or product contract.
  6. External capacity improves outcome or economics enough to justify the added entropy.

Ten landing questions, and why each one matters

QuestionWhy it matters
What changes state in this World?Defines events, transitions, decisions, opportunities, and timing.
Who owns or controls what?Identity, authority, incentives, consent, and transaction ability.
What is valuable to whom?Prevents optimizing one actor while destroying value elsewhere.
What evidence is trusted?Determines confidence, claims, qualification, and professional review.
What rules and risks bind action?Jurisdiction, licensing, safety, privacy, ethics, contracts, and irreversibility.
Where is supply, demand, or capacity fragmented?Reveals market or orchestration opportunities.
Which existing capabilities transfer?Reduces build time without assuming domain equivalence.
What must remain human or physical?Sets realistic automation and field requirements.
What outcome closes the loop?Prevents endless analysis and defines learning.
When does this become a product or World?Sets phase-transition gates and avoids metaphor inflation.

DIVISION VIII

Engineering contracts and emergence gates

Thirteen universal invariants, then the acceptance tests that prove them.
01

Identity is singular. One canonical identifier; multiple names and representations allowed.

02

State is explicit. Intended, declared, observed, verified, and deployed are not silently conflated.

03

Evidence is traceable and uncertainty survives compression.

04

Authority is bounded and credentials do not grant permission.

05

Resources are finite and economically accounted.

06

Context changes weight. No metric, model, or strategy dominates all Worlds.

07

Learning changes future behavior only after controlled evaluation and promotion.

08

Irreversibility requires stronger evidence and authority.

09

Every object has lifecycle, decay, retirement, and tombstone state.

10

Products and Worlds import core contracts; they do not fork the core.

11

Outcomes outrank persuasive narratives.

12

Negative evidence, disagreement, and failure remain visible.

13

Complexity must pay rent.

Engineering acceptance: seven tests, each with a pass condition

TestPass condition
Semantic zoomThe same object can be inspected from Universe to World to run to ingredient without losing identity or lineage.
No duplicate truthMetaphor views project canonical objects rather than creating separate state stores.

Emergence gateA product, World, or bloom claim requires repeatable outcome and lifecycle proof.
RetirementStale Worlds, recipes, providers, or nodes can be superseded or retired without erasing history.
