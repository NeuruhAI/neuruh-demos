3. Pricing simulator (MODELED, NOT A QUOTE)

Pick a category, type a list price, pick a markdown schedule. The page shows the modeled price step by step. Every dollar figure below comes from the number you typed or from the formula applied to it. The demo never emits a price on its own.


Formula (my design decision, not a paper formula). Modeled price at each step equals the list price multiplied by (1 minus each markdown applied so far). Markdowns compound multiplicatively: 10 percent off, then 25 percent off, leaves 67.5 percent of the list price, not 65 percent. No fees, no shipping, no tax, no haggling, and no sale probability are modeled. Sale timing is hypothetical: a markdown date is a schedule you pick, not a prediction that the item sells on that day. Every modeled figure is labeled MODELED, NOT A QUOTE. Nothing here is a valuation.
