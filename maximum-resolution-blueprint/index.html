1. What this demo is

The source blueprint converts "Adaptive Intelligence Thesis v0.1 from a coherent conceptual scaffold into a maximum-resolution, testable, machine-readable, visually navigable, and executable research operating system." Its operating rule is: No floating claims. Every claim must carry provenance, scope, confidence, falsifiers, and lineage. This demo implements three of its core instruments with the document's own content:

  • Gap tracker (Section 2 of the source): all 14 missing objects, each with a status and a severity, with working filters and a computed rollup.
  • Model explorer (Sections 3 to 7, 9, 12): the document's 7 formal models, each expandable with definition and parameters.
  • Build roadmap (Section 14): all 9 phases plus the 10 operator actions, with working progress state computed from checked steps.

Source claims used in this demo: 14 gaps, 7 formal models, 12 retrieval variables, 11 polarity variables, 12 capability axes, 9 capability tiers, 8 emergence scoring dimensions with a threshold of 18 of 24, 16 required services, 15 canonical visuals, 8 canon volumes, 12 priority experiments (E-01 to E-12), 15 risks (R-01 to R-15), a 180-day program in 9 phases, and a v0.2 release gate at Day 180.

    7. Try it: a walkthrough

    1. Filter the gaps. Set Severity to Critical. The table shows 3 rows: Identity / boundary, Causality, and Meaning / relevance. The rollup updates to match.
    2. Search. Clear the severity filter and type "retrieval" in Search. One row remains: Meta-learning, because its required treatment mentions "retrieval policy". Search covers the gap name, why it matters, required treatment, and provisional status. Try "uncertainty" for exactly 1 row.
    3. Open a model. Expand "Emergence Qualification Protocol" and check the threshold: total score 18 or more of 24, with no zero in persistence or robustness. That is a source claim, and the arithmetic is 8 dimensions times a maximum of 3 equals 24.
    4. Work the roadmap. Check Phase 0, Phase 1, and Phase 2. Progress reads 33.3 percent, which is 3 of 9 phases.
    5. Load the roadmap-complete sample. All 9 phases check and progress reads 100.0 percent.
    6. Share the state. Click "Generate share link", then "Copy share link". Open the link in a new tab: the same filters and checked phases are restored.

    8. How it works / What this is not

    How it works

    • All gap, model, phase, and action data lives in one JavaScript data block in this file, transcribed from the source PDF.
    • The rollup counts are computed from the gap data on every filter change, never hard-coded.
    • Roadmap progress is computed as checked steps divided by total steps, times 100, rounded to one decimal.
    • The share link is base64 JSON of the current state in the URL hash; on load the page decodes the hash and restores filters, checks, and expanded models.
    • The completeness meter is computed from a filled-fields count over a fixed field list.

    What this is not

    • Not the research OS itself. It is a demo of the tracker, explorer, and roadmap instruments only. No ingestion, atomization, ranking, or retrieval runs here.
    • Not empirical proof of Adaptive Intelligence. The source itself says it is a design specification, not empirical proof.
    • Not a live system. Nothing here connects to a corpus, a graph, or a service. There is no backend.
    • Not a validation of the formulas. The polarity, retrieval, and emergence functions are uncalibrated skeletons in the source.
    • Not canon. The source is raw material. Severity ratings and tracker statuses are demo assignments.

    9. Honest gaps

    What the source does not define, or what this demo could not verify:

    • Uncalibrated coefficients. The emergence function uses alpha, beta, gamma, delta, epsilon, and lambda, and the retrieval score uses weights w1 to w9. The source gives no values, no units, and no calibration data. The demo lists them as symbols only.
    • No units for polarity variables. P, C, F, A, M, G, T, D, L, R, and E are defined in words. Only P and E are given a 0 to 1 range. "Stakes" in PressureLoad is never defined.
    • Numbering errors in the source. Section 7.1 lists its 10 qualification criteria numbered 5 to 14. Section 8.3 lists its 10 canonicalization rules numbered 15 to 24. Section 14.2 lists its 10 operator actions numbered 25 to 34. The demo renumbers each list 1 to 10 and flags the original numbering here rather than silently fixing it.
    • Stale partnership framing. Section 3.4 uses "Jeramie and Bryan" as its partnership example and says the partnership "has emerged into a higher-order operating system." Per Jeramie's 2026-10-22 correction, DeedSonar is solely his and Bryan Franklin never signed. The source (July 2026) predates that correction; the demo keeps the section reference but does not repeat the partnership claim as current fact.
    • No evidence attached. The central principle example in Section 12 carries confidence claim 0.0 and extraction 1.0 in the source's own JSON. The demo reports that as a source claim, not as a verified fact.
    • Unverified counts. The demo transcribed the 15 visuals, 16 services, 12 experiments, and 15 risks from the source tables. The PDF text extraction preserved them, but no independent source was checked against them.
    • Layout not eyeballed. This page was tested in jsdom for behavior only. It was not viewed in a real browser, so visual layout is an accepted limit.
    Duplication flagged in the source: the polarity phase space, the emergence furnace, and the claim lifecycle appear both as figures and as tables with overlapping content; the "Meaning / relevance" gap and the "Value / utility" gap overlap (both ask why a distinction matters); and the 8-volume canon restates Sections 3 to 13 as volumes rather than adding new content. The demo keeps all three representations separate and does not merge them.
